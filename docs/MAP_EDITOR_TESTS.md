# The map maker's tests

Part of [The map maker](MAP_EDITOR.md): the rules of the editor and how the page is made are there. The list of its pull requests is in [MAP_EDITOR_HISTORY.md](MAP_EDITOR_HISTORY.md).

## Tests

`tests/scripts/test_web_mapmaker.py` (quick tier) runs `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_check.js` (the reader on all 540 maps of the library, the view, the sizes, the card, the colors; broken copies of the scripts must fail it) and pins the page's wiring. `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_edit_check.js` runs the drawing (the brush and the line, strokes in version 8 and 9, fill, undo and redo byte for byte, the bounded history, the dictionary, the palette against the game's list of names, the tools on a fake page with their keys, and hills, ants and food in version 8 and 9: where each goes, moving, the eraser, one step of the history, the bounds, a dictionary that is too short; the Bridge chip's place and its warning for pieces on dry ground, counted again after a stroke, the eraser, undo and redo and for a map that is opened); about 80 broken copies of the scripts must fail it, and `tests/data/mapmaker_bridge_mutants.json` (34 broken copies of the Bridge chip, run by the test and by `tools/mutate.py`) too; the browser part `panel` holds that the whole hint is inside the tools column's visible area at 1280 x 800, 1366 x 768 and 1280 x 720. `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_objects_check.js` holds the bigger objects and the solid bit to the 540 maps of the library: the table of solid tiles to the game's flags, the list of 29 to a measure of the library made again (the tiles, their names, their footprints), each of the 29 put in a free place on every map and taken away again (the cells, anchors, flags and solid bits as the library's own objects have them; one tap on any cell takes it whole; undo and redo are byte for byte; the layers are the level they were), the same on the maps converted to version 9, what is in the way, the edge of the map, a version 9 map wider than 256, a dictionary that is too short; Python counts the library's objects again with struct, and about 20 broken copies must fail it. `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_blocks_check.js` holds the lists to the 540 maps of the library (every list is read where it is and written back as the same bytes, a change and its undo give the bytes back, a hill taken away and put back is the same cells, nothing but what is named changes), and Python reads the same lists again with struct. `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_dialog_check.js` runs the dialogs on a small fake of the DOM (`web_mapmaker_fake_dom.js`: focus, Escape, Tab, New map; broken copies must fail it). `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_save_check.js` runs the store on a fake of the database (a draft is added and never written over, the slot of unsaved work, a browser that keeps nothing, the site's list of maps) and the Save dialog and the name check of Copy on the fake DOM; `tests/data/mapmaker_save_mutants.json` (about 50 broken copies, run by the test and by `tools/mutate.py`) must all fail it. `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_mine_check.js` runs My maps (the store's list and delete on the fake database, the list, its filters, Open, Copy of a saved map and the question before Delete on the fake DOM); `tests/data/mapmaker_mine_mutants.json` (38 broken copies; two judge the page's pinned source) must all fail it. `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_submit_check.js` runs Submit for approval and My submissions (the words, the sent store on a fake database, the dialog, what is sent and every answer of a fake door, the list, Withdraw, the status link); `tests/data/mapmaker_submit_mutants.json` (about 50 broken copies, including the tabs of My maps) must all fail it. `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_props_check.js` runs the properties dialog on the same fake with a stand-in for the core (the words, the counter in bytes, the box that stops, the core's sentence and Cut it, the steppers, the ant types, Apply and Cancel, text that is text; about 25 broken copies must fail it). The canvas and the page's start need a real browser: the opt-in check covers them. `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_core_check.js` holds the page's reading, and the maps it has drawn on, to the real module (the core reads, checks and writes an edited map as the same level, the objects keep their anchors through a conversion in both directions, undo gives back the cells of all 540 library maps, the checker says "Blue has no hill" until the hill is there and counts the ants and the piles that were put, writes the edited file as the very same bytes, and every whole hill of the library, as a Classic and as an Extended map, taken away and put back is the same level; all 29 bigger objects on a blank map of each kind, and three on each of the 540 maps, leave the checker's verdicts as they were, are written as the very same bytes, and keep their anchors through a conversion in both directions; the web job of CI runs it from the image). The properties on the real core are case I of `web_mapmaker_core_check.js` (all 540 maps as Classic and as Extended maps: the values a map has set again are the same bytes and no step of the history, other values are one step whose bytes are the core's, the grid is as it was, the check says what it said less a long description, undo and redo are byte for byte; 29 and 30 bytes, 255 and 256; the dialog on the real core). `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_convert_check.js` runs the arithmetic of the frame (on thousands of random pulls it stays on the map and within the limits), the names, the Crop to fit screen and every dialog of copy and convert on the same fake with a stand-in for the core (about 50 broken copies must fail it), and case J of `web_mapmaker_core_check.js` runs them on the real core (every map of the library goes up and comes back through the dialogs; the crop and the shortened description on a large Extended map). `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_ground_check.js` holds the five kinds and the 239 tiles to the game's names, the facts of the table and the 540 maps (the shapes), the palette and the flat colors, and the sheet of a kind on the fake DOM; case N of `web_mapmaker_edit_check.js` runs the chips, the sheet and any single tile through the Ground tool; `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_sprites_check.js` runs the sprite layer against the real atlas and a recording canvas (the ground and object draws, the anchors and the row order, the layer kept between frames, the pan, the stroke's mend, the zoom, the far toys, the ants and the ghost, the chips, the open map's dictionary, and a failed atlas); `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_names_check.js` holds the rule to the game's renderer for every cell of the 540 maps with an oracle written again from `renderer.cpp` (about 7,000 cells of 270 maps were drawn by number before: 0 now; `tests/data/mapmaker_names_mutants.json`); `tests/data/mapmaker_sprites_mutants.json` (about 60 broken copies, run by the test and by `tools/mutate.py`) must all fail them; `tests/scripts/test_map_atlas.py` builds the atlas and reads it back (every ground tile pixel for pixel, every object the editor offers pixel for pixel with all its parts put together by a second, independent painter, with its offset, a flower's order of parts, the library's objects, the sizes, a palette that is too big; `tests/data/map_atlas_mutants.json`, 7 broken copies of the tool, run by `tools/mutate.py`, must all fail it) and checks the places that carry it (the Dockerfile, `.dockerignore`, `build_web.sh`, nginx, the CI's check of the image, the notices). The opt-in browser check is `tests/scripts/test_web_mapmaker.sh` ([`TESTING_PYTHON.md`](TESTING_PYTHON.md)); its `sprites` part looks at the real pixels (and runs the page with the atlas refused), the other parts run with the atlas refused so that their pixel checks read the flat colors.
`tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_ants_check.js` (run by `test_web_mapmaker.py`): the Ants box on real library maps with the real reader, blocks and history, and a fake DOM: one ant put and taken (bytes, Undo, Redo, refusals), the mix (exact counts, chessboard, small box, one Undo), the clump (its bytes made by hand and a pinned digest, the nearest free cells, two clumps that both stay, 250 ants, one Undo for each, in the box on the fake page and at the 65,535 limit), Clear and the warnings; with `--write-maps DIR` it also writes two library maps with twelve big clumps, which `test_web_mapmaker.py` (`TheGameLoadsTheClumps`, when `map_sweep` is built) has the real loader and engine load, start and play; broken copies in `tests/data/mapmaker_ants_mutants.json` (`tools/mutate.py ... --keep-going --jobs 1`, the four `browser_` ones with `ANTS_WEB_URL`). The opt-in browser part `ants` (`web_mapmaker_browser_ants.py`) does it with real bytes on a 100 x 100 Extended map (the Mosh Pit with four hills and 4 x 25 mixed ants in the middle: the check says every team has a hill and ants and it works in the new version, whatever the rows) and TINY (still works in the original game), and a phone's 44 pixel buttons, and then the clump's player path on a desktop and on a phone held upright (two taps in separate places put two clumps and both stay, 250 ants typed in the number box, Undo and Redo one clump at a time, Pick up moves one, Erase takes one and Undo gives its bytes back exactly, and the map is not drawn stretched after a tool changed its room).
`tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_fill_check.js` (run by `test_web_mapmaker_fill.py`; the broken copies in `tests/data/mapmaker_fill_mutants.json` must all fail it, and `tools/mutate.py ... --keep-going --jobs 1` runs them too): Fill a box on real library maps with the real reader, blocks, editor and history and a fake DOM: every pattern against a list made again here (counted from the box's corner), the seed's repeatability and density, which cells are skipped and why (water and its two exceptions, a bridge piece on dry ground, solid ground, a rock, a hill, ants, a plant, a pile; Classic and Extended maps), the caps (250 ants, 5,000 of anything else, the room of the lists at 65,535), one step of the history that is the same bytes as putting the cells one by one with the page's own functions, byte for byte Undo and Redo, the box in the page (the words while it is dragged and after an Undo, a lost drag, Escape, a tap, the slider, the team and the ant types) and the switch in the tools (off at the start, remembered, only for Objects, Food and Ants, the painter's hand-over, a part that did not load). The AI's `fill_area` is case C of `web_mapmaker_ai_check.js` and `TheTools` of `test_hub_ai.py`; `web_mapmaker_extended.js` makes an Extended map of a library map for both checks.
Typing in the boxes (the name, note and maker's name of Submit, the description, minutes and eggs of the properties, the rows and columns of New map and Resize, the search of Open a map, the name of a copy or of Save as, the number boxes of the Ants box): a box is made once and a key never takes it out of the page (a browser takes the focus from a box that a script removes or moves, and cuts off an input method's composition with it); what is shown around a box is made again only when what it says has changed; and when the page changes what was typed (a line break, a text that does not fit, a letter in a number box) the caret stays where the person had it. The checks type through the fake DOM (`tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_fake_dom.js` blurs a box that is removed, puts the caret at the end when a value is set, stops a box at its maxlength and has `typeKey`, `typeCheck`, `caretCheck` and `composeCheck`), each in the check of its dialog; `tests/data/mapmaker_focus_mutants.json` (broken copies, run by `test_web_mapmaker.py` and by `tools/mutate.py ... --keep-going --jobs 1`) must all fail them. The opt-in `tests/scripts/test_web_mapmaker_focus.sh` (`web_mapmaker_focus_browser.py`; a Chromium-based browser and python3, no built core) types in a real browser with its own keys and input method on the real scripts, and `tests/data/mapmaker_focus_browser_mutants.json` must fail it. The map's name box of the top bar (`#map-name`) is a box of the page's own markup that nothing makes again (the page writes it only when a map is opened, with the text it already has) and needs the core to start, so these checks do not type in it.

`tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_line_check.js` (run by `test_web_mapmaker.py`; 32 broken copies in `tests/data/mapmaker_line_mutants.json`, all caught): the straight line on the real canvas, tools and history with a fake DOM: the rule for Ground, Objects and Erase against a list made by hand and against the rule written again (across, down, the longer side, a tie across, the first cell alone, the line kept, the way back, the four edges with the status line and the place the stroke goes on from), Shift, Ctrl, both, Command and Alt alone, the keys let go and pressed in a stroke, a zoomed and panned view, the same bytes as a free stroke along the line (brushes of 1, 3 and 5, Auto edges, Fill, Undo), the same bytes as before with no key for a mouse, a pen and a finger, and the tools that are not strokes; the browser part `line` of `web_mapmaker_browser.py` (`web_mapmaker_browser_line.py`) does it with real mouse events.

`tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_minimap_check.js` (run by `test_web_mapmaker.py`; 82 broken copies in `tests/data/mapmaker_minimap_mutants.json`, all caught): the small map on a recording canvas and fake boxes: the hills' size (at least 10 pixels at 31 to 480 cells a side), center, edge and color, Hide and Show map, Lock, the place (drag, keys, Home, two taps, a smaller room, a stored place that does not fit, a storage that throws); `web_mapmaker_browser.py --only minimap` (opt-in) does the same in a real browser, with a mouse and a finger.

`tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_pickup_check.js` (run by `test_web_mapmaker.py`; `--quick` looks at 40 maps of the library): the Pick up tool on made-up maps (version 8 and 9) and on all 540 maps of the library, with the real reader, blocks and history and a STRICT fake DOM (a node list has no filter, map or forEach, and an element throws when its innerHTML is set): what stands on a cell and what it is called (a hill, a pile with its stage object, a plant, a stack of ants, what is under what), an ant's group and an area, every reason that a ghost is red in its plain words (the edge, a hill, an object, ants, a pile, a plant, solid ground, a Classic map's one byte, a hill that is never copied, the 65,535 of a list), water as a hint and never a refusal, and a lift on a red place that puts the thing back with its reason, the change of a move, a copy and a delete (the anchors of version 8 and 9, the solid bits, a bomb's owner word and a neutral bomb's 0x0101, a pile's bites and points, a plant's dropper, an ant's own type, ONE step, byte for byte Undo and Redo), a whole area as one step, 5,000 ants moving together, the bar and its buttons, the nudge arrows, the keys, the pointer (a tap, a press that drags, a box), the ghost and the ring drawn on the canvas over the map (a recording canvas: green and red, "door" and "from"; the map itself is not painted for them), a map that changes under a pick, the tool through the page's own `Tools` (loaded on demand, a part that did not load, Pan), text of a file that never shows (a dictionary with markup), and a 512 x 512 map with 20,000 ants; the library sweep picks every kind of thing of every map, moves it to the nearest free place, copies it and deletes it, and holds the bytes to a second reading of the file format written apart (775 of 961 things in the quick run are of the format's own shape; the rest are old maps' quirks and are held to the looser checks), and, when a built map core is there (`--module DIR`; the web job of CI runs it from the image), the core's own check says nothing new after any of them. The library files on the disk are hashed before and after and never written. `tests/data/mapmaker_pickup_mutants.json` (194 broken copies of the script and its hooks, ten more that only the real browser notices; `tools/mutate.py` runs them too) must all fail it. The opt-in browser check (`test_web_mapmaker.sh`, part `pickup`, `web_mapmaker_browser_pickup.py`) puts the tool through real clicks, keys and touches at 1280 x 800 (a mouse), 390 x 844 (a finger), 844 x 390 and 1280 x 600 on a Mosh Pit made with struct (and, at 1280 x 800, 1000 x 700 and 390 x 844, holds the map above the bar: its last row panned above the bar, a thing picked at the foot in view, the top row kept as the bar comes and goes; at 1280 x 800 and 1000 x 700 a mouse that goes over the last rows 2 pixels at a time does not make the map grow and shrink at every step; a phone turned from sideways to upright with the bar open gets the bar's new height as the room below the page) (the gold ring and the ghost's outline are read from the overlay canvas's pixels, the bytes that Download hands over are read back with struct, a button is 44 pixels and an arrow 48, the bar is at the foot of a phone's screen) and saves the pictures M1 to M7 (and S2, the bar of a short screen) with `--shots`.


`tests/scripts/test_web_mapmaker_play.py` (quick tier) runs `tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_play_check.js` (the verdict on made-up answers of the check and on the real core and all 540 maps, the start rules on all 625 choices, the game page's whitelist and hand-over, the strip and the way back, the dialogs on a fake page; 152 broken copies in `tests/data/mapmaker_play_mutants.json` must each fail a check, and `tools/mutate.py` runs the same list with `--keep-going --jobs 1`) and pins the page's wiring; the part `play` of the browser check (`tests/scripts/web_mapmaker_play_browser.py`, opt-in) starts a real game from the editor, plays it (the roster, the ants, the clock on the screen), compares the bytes that the game got with the file's own, goes back and compares the editor's bytes and history.
