# The Discord pictures of the chat hub

The hub keeps one small copy of the Discord profile picture of an account that is logged in, shows its public name to the pages, and serves it from its own address: a visitor's browser never talks to Discord for it, and no Discord id, no picture code and nothing made from them reaches a page. This document is the part of [HUB.md](HUB.md) that belongs to the pictures: the fetch, the cleanup, the frames, the switch, the operator's block, the limits, the table, the log and the nginx door.

## The fetch, the copy and its end

After a login has been answered, the hub fetches one small copy of the account's Discord picture, in the background (`hub/pictures.py`, `hub/discord.py` `picture()`): the login never waits for it and never fails because of it. The address is `https://cdn.discordapp.com/avatars/<id>/<code>.png?size=64`, made in one place from constants and the two parts, each matched against its shape right there (an id is digits, a code is 32 hex digits, `a_` before them for an animated one, whose `.png` is the still picture); a part of another shape makes no request at all. The call is the login's own client: https, certificate checked, 3 seconds to connect and 6 in all, no redirect followed, 49,152 bytes read in all and 32,768 kept, no cookie or token sent. It is kept only when the status is 200, the type is exactly `image/png` and the file is a PNG whose every chunk is whole (lengths fit, CRCs match), whose header comes first and says 8 to 128 pixels and a depth the color allows, and that ends with IEND and nothing after it, and whose data is a whole zlib stream that inflates to exactly the rows that the header says (never inflated past that). The hub then writes its own copy: the header, PLTE, tRNS, the data and the end, nothing else (text, EXIF, color profiles and animation chunks are dropped); the pixels are not decoded. Any other outcome keeps the letter and leaves an older copy as it was, but a 404, which deletes it. No retry: the next login is the retry, and a new login always gets its fetch. An account has one fetch waiting or running at a time (a login that comes while it is on its way gets none of its own: logins that come meanwhile are one), two run at a time, 32 wait (the rest are dropped), a burst of 5 and then one a second for the whole hub; a dropped fetch is counted and told, at most every 10 seconds, as `picture kind=busy dropped=<count>`.

The copy is a row of the additive table `pictures` in `hub.db` (below), by the account's reference, with a random 128-bit name that is new at every login and made from nothing of the account, the code or the bytes. `GET /hub/pic/<name>.png` answers it: `image/png`, `nosniff`, `default-src 'none'; sandbox`, `Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy: same-origin`, `private, max-age=86400, immutable`, the same on every answer; a name that is not 32 lower-case hex digits, is not known, is not shown or is past its login's 30 days is a JSON 404 (`no-store`); 20 a second for an address (a burst of 240). Anyone who has the name may fetch it: it cannot be guessed. It is deleted with the last logout, a kick, Forget me, a switch to off or a block (a person's `off` is remembered 400 days after the last login; a block stays, like a ban), a ban, and by the hourly purge when the login ran out: the store's rule is no login, no picture, so whatever ends an account's last login (a logout, the callback that replaces a browser's login of another account, the purge, the cap of logins) takes the picture with it. A fetch that ends after any of these keeps nothing, and a picture's time is cut to the end of the account's last login when it is kept, and again to the latest end of the logins that are left whenever one of them ends, so that it is never served after the last login has run out, purge or no purge. No picture is fetched for a switched off, blocked or banned account. At most 2,000 pictures and 8 MiB: a new one that does not fit takes the room of the ones fetched longest ago. Nothing derived from an id is in a name, a table, a log line or an error; the log says `picture kind=ok|none|down|type|big|shape` (`busy` with the count of dropped fetches, `bug` with the class of an error) and nothing else. nginx keeps the answer's file for as long as the address is asked for (it goes five minutes after the last request) though it is not served after its five minutes.

## Frames

Protocol version 1 stays: everything here is additive, and a page that does not know a field or a frame ignores it. A picture name is always exactly 32 lower-case hex digits, present only where the account has a picture that may be shown now. There is no picture in a chat line and no frame of its own for a picture: the lines are as they were.

| Where | Field or frame | When it is there |
|---|---|---|
| `you` (the welcome, the `you` frame, `/hub/me`) | `pics`: `"on"`, `"off"` or `"blocked"` | for an account that is logged in, on a hub that has the Discord login; never for a guest and never on a hub without the login |
| the same `you` | `pic`: the name | only while the account has a picture that may be shown (`pics` is `on`, the copy is kept and its login has not run out) |
| a row of the list of players ([HUB.md](HUB.md#presence)) | `pic`: the name | the same rule; a change of it is a changed row, sent by the list's own diff within its pace; a banned account is not in the list at all |
| down `{"t":"you","you":{...}}` | the whole `you` again | to every socket of an account, and to nobody else, when its picture arrives or goes where the account's sockets stay open (the fetch is done, the switch, the operator's block or lift, a ban from the terminal, the end of a picture's time). After the last logout, a kick, a ban from the chat, Forget me and a switch of the shown name the account's sockets are CLOSED (1000) and no `you` is sent: the page connects again and reads the welcome |
| up `{"t":"pics","on":true\|false}` | the switch | a guest is answered `err` `login`; anything else that is wrong (not a boolean, past the limit, a blocked picture) `err` `pics`, a mistake of the socket (so a page never retries by itself: twenty in a minute close the socket); a request for the state that the account is in already changes nothing, costs a token as any other, and is answered by the whole `you` to the asking socket only, so that a page's box never waits for nothing; a burst of 3 and then one every 30 seconds for an account across all its sockets; a hub without the Discord login does not know the frame (`err` `bad`, as any other unknown one) |

Off deletes the copy and its name first, then the pages are told (the old address answers 404 at once; the nginx cache may still hold it for up to five minutes); on shows nothing until the next login (the page offers a trip through the Discord login) and asks for no fetch; only a fetch of the login that was still on its way when the person switched off and on again goes on, and its picture then appears (the person's last word). A block by the operator wins over the switch, survives a login and Forget me (one row stays: the reference, a flag and the time, no id and no name; its log rows go in 30 days), and is not the person's to lift. A person who only logs out keeps the mark of an `off` for 400 days after the last login. A moderator has no command for a picture in this release: a kick takes the picture with the login, a ban for good, and the operator can block one.

The pictures' list is read by the hub's clock once a tick (and at once after anything here that changes a picture): when the store's counter says that something changed, here or in another process (`hub.admin`), or when the soonest picture has run out. A welcome, a `you` and a row are made from that list and read no database. One look reads the counter, the names, the marks and the soonest end in one read transaction, and a `you` or a row never carries a name for an account whose picture is off or blocked. **How long a hidden name can still be told:** 0 seconds in `you` for everything the hub itself does, at most one tick after a change by `hub.admin` in another process or the end of a picture's time, at most the list's pace plus one tick (3 seconds) in a row of the list, with no bound while the database refuses the hub's reads (one line of the log every 10 seconds); the hub's own address answers 404 from the moment of the delete, and only the nginx cache can answer for up to five minutes. Before the first look a database that refuses leaves "no pictures" for that look and the hub keeps serving. A look costs the whole table of pictures (about 190 ms at the cap of 100,000 marks, on the hub's one thread, at every change): a known limit. A forged `pic`, `pics` or `on` in any other frame (`say`, `here`, `who`, `pick`, `ping`) is read by nobody and changes nothing.

## The operator

`docker exec ants-hub python -m hub.admin nopic ACCOUNT` blocks the account's picture on this site and `pic ACCOUNT` lifts the block (ACCOUNT as in [HUB.md](HUB.md#moderation): a username, a reference or an id; only the reference is ever printed). Both are written to the moderators' log as `nopic` and `pic`, the actor `terminal`, the target by reference. The running hub reads the change at its next tick and tells the account's sockets. After Forget me the account's other rows are gone and the block's own row has only the reference: `pic REFERENCE` finds it there (the name and the id do not). `hub.admin status` has a line `pictures: N of 2,000 kept, X of 8.0 MB; N switched off, N blocked`.

## Limits

| What | Limit |
|---|---|
| the pictures | one fetch at a time for an account, 2 at a time and 32 waiting, a burst of 5 and then one a second for the hub, 3 seconds to connect and 6 in all, 49,152 bytes read and 32,768 kept, 2,000 pictures and 8 MiB; `/hub/pic/`: 20 a second for an address (a burst of 240, in nginx and in the hub) |
| the switch | `pics`: a burst of 3, then one every 30 seconds, for an account across all its tabs |

## Data

**The `pictures` table is additive too** (`ref` the account's keyed reference, `show` 1, 0 for the person's off or 2 for a block, `name`, `png`, `until` the end of the login that fetched it, `at`): no version of the schema came with it (`user_version` stays 4), a hub of the version before opens the same file and never looks at it, and its Forget me does not delete a picture, so the purge deletes every one past `until` and `GET /hub/pic/` serves none past it; after a rollback that is for good, with the hub stopped, `python -c "import sqlite3; c=sqlite3.connect('/data/hub.db'); c.execute('PRAGMA secure_delete=ON'); c.execute('DROP TABLE pictures'); c.commit()"` (the pragma first, so that the bytes are zeroed whatever this SQLite is built with).

## Logs and the site's nginx

The log says `picture` with a kind (`ok`, `none`, `down`, `type`, `big`, `shape`, `busy` with the count of dropped fetches, `bug` with the class of the error) and nothing else. Nothing derived from an id is in any line.

A seventh door, `/hub/pic/<name>.png`, is the one regular expression (`^/hub/pic/[0-9a-f]{32}\.png\z`, before the locations of the static images): a GET without a query, a request of another site (`Sec-Fetch-Site` `cross-site` or `same-site`) is 403, no cookie and no header of the visitor's reaches the hub, no access log, 20 a second for an address (a burst of 240; 429), and the answer is kept 5 minutes for everybody (`proxy_cache`, a 404 is not kept), so the hub is asked once for a picture in five minutes whatever the number of viewers; the hub's headers pass and its `nosniff` is hidden so that each is there once; a picture that was deleted can still be answered from the cache for up to 5 minutes. The page policy is unchanged (`img-src 'self'`).

## Tests

`tests/scripts/test_hub_pictures.py` holds the fetch, the store and the door of this document against a fake picture host (the PNG check on hand-made bad files, odd ids and codes that make no request, a redirect, a flood, every failure leaving the letter, the name at every login, each way a picture ends, the door's headers, the log read for canaries, the real process) and `test_nginx_hub_pic.py` the nginx door, run for real; `tests/data/hub_pictures_mutants.json` breaks each of those rules.

`tests/scripts/test_hub_pictures_frames.py` holds the frames and the switch on real sockets (`you` for a member, a guest and a hub without the login, `/hub/me`, the rows of the list, the picture arriving and going told to the account's sockets only, the switch with every refusal, off before any frame, on with no fetch, the block against the switch, a login and Forget me, a kick, a ban, the last logout, the time of a login, the operator's commands, every frame of every kind of socket read for an id, a hash of it, a reference and the picture's code, forged fields), and the same file's mutants are in `tests/data/hub_pictures_mutants.json`.
