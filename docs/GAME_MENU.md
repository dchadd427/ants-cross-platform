# The settings menu

**Esc**, or a **Settings** button, opens a panel over the running game: Sound, Video, Controls and Game, with **Resume** and **Leave game**. The game keeps running behind it (the panel says so). This page is the one home of the menu: what it does, the one table of rows that every look of it is drawn from, and how the browser's panel is built. The keys it lists are in [`CONTROLS.md`](CONTROLS.md), the browser page around it in [`PLAY_IN_BROWSER.md`](PLAY_IN_BROWSER.md), the touch screen in [`TOUCH.md`](TOUCH.md).

## Opening and closing

- **Esc closes or cancels what is open first, and opens the menu only when there is nothing to cancel.** A dialog (a team-up offer, the quit question, the quick help) answers its own Esc as ever; the original's options window keeps its Esc (it does nothing) and the menu never opens over it; with something selected (ants, the hill) or a pedestal latched, Esc deselects as the original does, and the **next** Esc opens the menu. That is the rule of the HUD (`HUD::handle_key_down`): the original's Esc is unchanged, the menu is what an Esc that would have done nothing does.
- **A held Esc does not open or close it**: only a first press (not a key repeat) counts, so that holding Esc to leave fullscreen in Chrome and Edge does not flicker it.
- **The Settings button** is for what has no Esc: in the page's header right after Menu (**one button at every width**: with the word "Settings" from 1140 px wide, and only the gear below that, so that the header stays one row on a 360 px phone with Menu, Fullscreen, Settings and More; it is a real button named "Settings", 44 px high, and in a match opens the same menu as Esc), and in the bar of a recording. There is no strip under the picture any more: the Picture shape and the Fullscreen mouse live here, in Video; the one other place is a recording's bar, which keeps its Picture button (a small window with the same dropdown). **Nothing is laid over the game's picture, fullscreen or not** (the maintainer's word): in fullscreen, real or the page's own, there is no Settings button on any device. A computer opens the menu with Esc; in Safari and Firefox, where Esc belongs to the browser in fullscreen, Esc leaves fullscreen first. **A phone in fullscreen has no way to open the menu** (no Esc, no button) until it leaves fullscreen; if that is wanted back, the button over the picture at the top is the one to restore (`git log -S"settings-btn-fs"`), or a button in the game's own bar (the known gap of the header's overhaul). **The button is never off once it is there and the game runs**: in a match with nothing waiting it opens the full menu; on every other screen (the opening picture, the quick help at the start, the game's own setup and room screens, the results, a match under a dialog or the countdown) it opens the **small form**, below. It is off (and says "Not now") only on a page that has none of the page's own rows to offer. **A focused gear presses itself with Enter or Space** (the game's window system would take the key, which is START on its setup screen, and cancel the browser's own press), opens the panel, and the key is the panel's from then on. **Esc** (the game's own request, `window.antsGameMenuAsk`) opens the full menu only, never the small form: Esc on the setup, results and opening screens is what it was.
- **Resume**, **Esc** and the cross (a phone has no Esc) close it. A click on the dimmed area does not: a stray touch cannot close it.
- **The small form**: the same frame (the title, the Esc hint, the cross), no tab column, the page's own rows in the table's order under their section's heading (**Fullscreen**, **Picture shape**, **Fullscreen mouse**; the last not on a touch screen and not without a pointer lock), one quiet line ("Sound, ant health, controls and the game's own settings are here during a match."; on a recording's page it says what is true there: the controls are here while the recording plays; the line is the dialog's description for a screen reader) and **Close**. It has no row of the game, no Controls list and no Leave game, and nothing in it calls `ants_menu_set` or `ants_menu_do`. It opens at the panel's own place, centered over the picture. The focus starts on Close. When the full menu becomes possible (the match begins, the countdown ends, the dialog is answered) the small form closes itself, without asking for the pointer lock, and the next press opens the full menu; while the screen changes among the others (opening picture, quick help, setup, results) it stays. In fullscreen there is no gear on any screen, so outside a match a player leaves fullscreen first.
- **A dialog that needs an answer closes the full menu** and the full menu does not open over one (the small form does): a team-up offer, a vote (F2 / F3), a lost link or the catch-up screen, the results, the quick help and the options window that the panel itself opens. The answer is more urgent than a setting, and a dialog drawn on the canvas would sit hidden under the panel.
- Where it is not: the frame of a match page (`play?replay=...&embed=1`, "Show replay") has no panel, no button and loads no script; Esc is what it was. The page's download has no button yet.

## What the game does while it is open

The panel is a child of the stage (`#game-stage`), the element that goes fullscreen, and fixed over the whole window, so it is there in fullscreen too, and its dimmed area covers the whole screen. While it is up:

- the game takes **no key, text, click, wheel or finger** (`Application::game_menu_takes`, told by `ants_menu_open`) and the map does not scroll at the edges; what a drag or a held button began before it opened is let go; the simulation, the sound and the network run on and nothing is sent;
- closing takes effect after the events that were queued, so the Esc that closes it is not a second Esc for the game;
- the page behind it is `inert`, Tab stays inside, the focus starts on Resume and goes back to the canvas when it closes, and the dialog is named (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, the tabs a `tablist`, the sliders real `input type=range`, the Picture shape a native `select`);
- the **pointer lock is let go** (a locked pointer has no cursor) and asked for again when it closes, if it was held or the page is fullscreen; a lock that the page is given while the panel is up (fullscreen asks for one) is let go at once: the panel hears it on the window in the capture phase, because the page's own handler there stops the event before the document's, and the 250 ms poll catches the rest.
- in the full menu only the keys of the panel's own controls (Tab, Enter, Space, the arrows, Home, End and Esc) stop at the panel; every other key goes on to the page, which keeps the browser's reactions to Ctrl+S, F1 and the like off, and the game takes none while the panel is up. The `keypress` of Enter and Space stops too (the game's window system cancels `keypress`, which would stop a native press of Enter on a focused button). The lifts of keys are always left alone.
- **in the small form no key press and no typed text reaches the game**: the game's setup, opening and quick-help screens act on keys before the game's gate for an open panel (S starts a match there, Q and X quit, T opens the room's chat line), so every `keydown` except a bare modifier (Shift, Control, Alt, AltGraph, Meta, OS, CapsLock, NumLock, ScrollLock, Fn) and every `keypress` stops at the document in the capture phase, ahead of the game's listeners on the window. A bare modifier passes so that the game's idea of what is held stays right (the price: one ends the opening picture early, as any key does). Before it stops a key that is not the panel's own, the panel asks the page's canceller (`cancelsKey`, `ANTS_PAGE.cancelsBrowserKey`) and cancels the browser's reaction when it says so (no Save page on Ctrl+S, no help on F1). The game is told too (`ants_menu_open`): on the results screen and under a dialog its own gate ignores input, and on every screen fingers and edge scrolling. This rests on the game listening for keys on the window in the bubble phase. The focus goes back to the canvas when it closes, or to the Settings button when a key opened it. **The Picture shape dropdown** is the page's own picker (`web/shape_picker.js`: a button that shows the shape and a list of the table's rows inside the panel, so it opens in fullscreen and under the fullscreen mouse lock, which a browser's native list does not; its button is in the panel's focus order, so Tab wraps around it). On the closed button Enter, Space, ArrowDown and ArrowUp open the list and no key changes the picture, on every browser alike; in the open list the arrows, Home, End and a typed letter move the mark, and Enter or Space (or a click or a tap on a row) chooses it: the picture of the running game changes at once and the panel stays open. Esc with the list open closes the list alone (the picker takes the key before the panel does); Esc on the closed button closes the panel.
- **A key whose press the panel kept stays the panel's until its lift**, in both forms: every key the panel stops (Esc, its own keys, and in the small form all the others) is remembered by its code, **if the panel saw its press** (a repeat is no press: a key that went down before the panel opened, and repeats while the panel is up, is stopped like any other but is not kept, so its lift reaches the game, which saw the press; without the lift the game would take the key's next press for a repeat and ignore it, Esc's request of the menu among others). Its repeats, the text of its press and its lift stop at the document too, also after the panel has closed (the listeners stay until the lift), and a repeat of Enter or Space is cancelled for the browser. Without this a key held when the panel closes goes on repeating, and the game's window system takes a repeat of a key it never saw go down for a new press (Enter held on Close would press START on the setup screen, Esc held would close the quick help or open the full menu again, Enter held on Forfeit would skip the results). The lift is the panel's for a second reason: the game cancels every key lift, which would stop a browser's press of a focused button at Space's lift. A fresh press of the key (no repeat) ends the hold, so a lift that was lost cannot keep a key from the game; a key that went down before the panel opened, and a bare modifier, keep their lifts. The holds stay when the panel is opened again before the key is lifted: the key is still held, and its repeats must not press the new panel's Close. Nothing else is held back from the game after the close.

## The rows

The small form has the three rows with `page: 1` that the page serves. The rows are **one table in C++** (`include/ants_app/game_menu.hpp`, `src/ants_app/game_menu.cpp`): id, name, section, kind, range, default, which screens show it, and every word. The page gets it as one JSON text from the game (`Module._ants_menu_describe`) and draws what it says; a skin of the desktop game can read the same table. `tests/data/game_menu_match.json` and `game_menu_watch.json` are what the game says, kept equal to the table by `tests/test_app/test_game_menu.cpp` (`test_game_menu --write-fixtures` writes them again), and the page's check draws from them.

| Section | Row | Where its value lives | Applies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sound | **Master volume** (new) | `master=` in the settings file; default 100, so nothing changes for anybody | while dragged (`AudioMixer::set_output_gain`, a 12 ms ramp: no click) |
| Sound | Music volume | the original's `Music Volume` | at the release, as the options window does |
| Sound | Sound effects volume | the original's `Sound Volume` | at the release, with the test voice |
| Sound | **Mute everything** (new) | `mute=on` / `off` | at once; every slider stays where it is and a muted sound goes on playing |
| Video | Fullscreen | the page | the page's own enter / leave (a real click: the browser's permission) |
| Video | Picture shape | the page (`ants.aspect.v2`, the address) | the page's picker (`web/shape_picker.js`, made from the page's table, `web/shapes.js`: the shape and its canvas, a quiet line under it names the shape that fills the screen); a choice calls the page's one function (the address and the stored choice follow); the row's range and words (their number) are made from the game's shape table (`kAspectTable`) |
| Video | Fullscreen mouse | the page (`ants.pointerlock`) | Locked / Free; not on a touch screen |
| Video | **Ant health** (new: Hurt ants, All ants, Off) and **Show health as** (new: Bar, Number) | `ant_health=` and `ant_health_style=` (Ctrl+L flips Off and the last choice that was on); [what they draw](VIEW_AND_HUD.md#ant-health-marks) | at once |
| Video | Map scroll speed | the original's `Scroll Speed` | at the release |
| Controls | the mouse (or the fingers) and the keys | none: a list to read, in `web/game_menu.js` | |
| Game | Participate in chat, Show quick help at startup | the original's names | at once |
| Game | Quick help (F1), Original options window (Ctrl+O) | none | close the panel and open the original's window |
| Game | Version | none | |

Master volume and the three that are the original's use the same numbers as the original's options window (0 to 99 on the slider; the stored 100 reads as 99), and the panel and the window show the same state, so they always agree. The values are the player's own: **Clear Cache & Update** erases the settings file and they come back to their defaults. The original's options window, Ctrl+O and the top bar's buttons are unchanged.

## Leave game

It asks first, in the words of what will happen (`game_menu::leave_kind`, the rules of the quit question's Yes, `Application::confirm_quit`; the default is **Stay in the game** and Esc means stay):

| Case | Question | Button |
|---|---|---|
| exactly one opponent left in a running match | "Forfeit and leave?" It ends now for everybody and counts as a loss for you. | Forfeit |
| two or more opponents in a network match | "Leave the game?" You will drop out; your ants are lost, the others play on, you cannot come back. | Leave game |
| a game at home against several, or alone | "Leave the game?" This match ends. Your game is not saved. | Leave game |
| the match is held (a seat missing, the countdown, a lost link) | "Leave the game?" The match is held. You leave it for good and cannot come back to this match. | Leave game |

A recording asks nothing (below). Only a click leaves: a question that is up stays when the match becomes held (a seat's link dropped) or the number of opponents changes under it (it is drawn again in the new words), and never turns into a leave by itself. Which kinds ask is the table's word (`asks`), not a rule of the page.

There is no separate Surrender: the forfeit is the quit that the game always had. Leaving goes through `confirm_quit`, so the `Quit` command, a drop out, the recording and the state hashes are what they were. A game on this computer leaves for the front page (a game in the lobby's frame, for that page's setup screen), as Quit always did.

## A recording or a live match that is watched

The same panel, without what a seat needs: no Game section, a tag ("Watching a replay", "Watching live"), the note "The replay keeps playing. Pause it with the bar.", and **Back to the matches** for Leave game, which asks nothing (nothing is lost) and goes to the list. Esc, Resume and the dimmed area behave as in a match. Its Settings button is in the replay's bar.

## How the browser's panel is built

- **The game's side** (`src/ants_app/application_game_menu.cpp`): the exports `ants_menu_describe`, `ants_menu_get`, `ants_menu_set` (a third number says whether to write the value now: a slider that is dragged is heard at once and written at the release), `ants_menu_do` and `ants_menu_open`; the HUD's Esc hook (`HUD::set_on_menu_request`) that calls `window.antsGameMenuAsk` of the page (a page without the script, or a frame, has none and Esc stays what it was); the four new settings (`game_menu::MenuSettings`).
- **The page's side** (`web/game_menu.js`, `web/game_menu.css`, beside `web/shell.html` like `countdown_button.js`): fetched once the game runs (`antsLoadGameMenu` in the page; retried once, then said in the toast, and Esc keeps its old meaning), never in an embedded frame and not in the first load. It makes its own Settings buttons and panel (every text with `textContent`), fetches its own stylesheet, and asks the game for everything that is the game's. ES5, no module system.
- **Scrolling**: the panel is held to the window and the section's list (`.gm-pane`) is its one scrolling box, so a list taller than it (the Video section on every computer, the Controls list) is moved with a wheel or a finger. In fullscreen the stage cancels the wheel over the black bars (`web/shell.html`, "THE BARS"); the panel is a child of the stage and is not a bar, so only a Ctrl + wheel over it is cancelled there.
- **Size**: the two files together are held to 53,000 bytes (15,500 gzipped; about 48,200 and 13,800 now) by `tests/scripts/test_web_front.py`, apart from the `web/front` folder's cap.
- **Tests**: `tests/test_app/test_game_menu.cpp` (the table, Leave game's kinds, the settings, Esc's rule, the input the game does not take, the acts; one case in `test_replay_app.cpp` for a recording, one in `test_app_integration_basics.cpp` for the mixer's gain), `tests/scripts/test_web_game_menu.py` with `web_game_menu_check.js` (the page's script on a strict fake of the DOM, with the picker's keys, and a look that the style and the script have nothing over the picture, in the quick tier) and the opt-in `tests/scripts/test_web_game_menu.sh` (a real browser, with the profile `outside` for the small form: the acceptance procedure above; the other profiles: 1280 x 800 with a pointer lock that comes while the panel is up, the Picture shape's list by keys and by mouse, a phone at 390 x 844 with a finger held on the map as the panel opens, and on its side, a recording, a frame; the `scroll` profile moves every list that is taller than its pane to its end with a wheel at 1280 x 720, 1024 x 600, 800 x 500 and in fullscreen, and with a finger and a wheel at 390 x 844 and 844 x 390). Its mutants are `tests/data/game_menu_mutants.json`. The page's side of the two rows that are its own (Picture shape, Fullscreen mouse): `tests/scripts/web_menu_handover_check.js` runs the page's call of `AntsGameMenu.forPage` and fails when it stops handing the menu `pickShape`, `lock` or `cancelsKey`, `web_edge_check.js` runs the `antsLock` block, and their mutants are `tests/data/pointer_lock_mutants.json`. `tests/scripts/web_picture_in_settings_check.js` (with `test_web_picture_in_settings.py`; skipped without Playwright, which CI does not install) runs the game page in Chromium with the game stood in for: the box inside the window with its ring and the side space under it (also with the rematch strip shown), the one Settings button after Menu at 360, 390, 844 and 1280 px, the header on one row, a tap that opens the menu, the Picture shape row in a game, a replay and a live match, and the Fullscreen mouse row.

## Acceptance procedure: the small form against a built game

A person with a keyboard, a Chromium-based browser and a built game (the beta site, or a local build; about ten minutes). Where the game is can be read in the browser's console: `Module._ants_match_running()` is 1 in a match, `Module._ants_menu_get(100)` is -1 when no match is on screen and 0 in a match, `Module._ants_menu_get(101)` is 1 while a dialog waits, `Module._ants_chat_probe(5)` is the length of the chat line. "Hold" means two full seconds. Steps 1 to 14b (with 13b) are the opt-in `tests/scripts/web_game_menu_check.py --profile outside` (run by `test_web_game_menu.sh`), so a build can be checked without a person; steps 15 to 17 are by hand.

1. Open `/play` with no map in the address. Wait for "Quick Help Game Instructions". The gear is on; its tooltip says "Settings: picture shape and fullscreen mouse".
2. Click the gear. The small form shows Fullscreen, Picture shape, Fullscreen mouse (not on a touch screen), one line and Close; the focus is on Close.
3. Move the focus off Close (click Locked). Type `c x q s`, Enter, Space. The quick help must not close.
4. Press Esc briefly. The form closes and the quick help is still there.
5. Click the gear and hold Esc. The quick help must not close.
6. Press C. The quick help closes and "Host Game Set-Up" shows (keys reach the game when no form is up). Note the map's name.
7. Click the gear, move the focus off Close, type `s q x t hello`, Enter, Space, Down, Down, F1, Backspace. No match starts, the game does not leave, the map does not change, no chat line opens. A short Esc closes the form and the screen is the same.
8. Click the gear and choose another Picture shape. The picture changes at once and the setup screen is drawn right. A click on the dimmed picture where START is does nothing. Click Close.
9. Click the gear (the focus is on Close) and press Space. The form closes.
10. Click the gear and hold Enter. The match must not start.
11. Press S. The match starts (the control: it was the setup screen all along).
12. Press Ctrl+Q (the quit question). Click the gear: the small form. Move the focus off Close and type `y`, Enter, `n`. The question must not be answered. A short Esc closes the form and the question still waits. Press N: it goes.
13. Press Esc: the full menu with its four tabs, the focus on Resume. Press Enter (it closes) and Esc again, then Space (it closes). Press Esc and hold it: the menu closes and stays closed.
13b. In a match hold Esc for a second so that it opens the menu, close the menu with Resume, then press Esc once. The menu must open (a key that went down before the panel opened keeps its lift).
14. Click the gear: the full menu, not the small form. Game, Leave game, Forfeit: the results come. Click the gear: the small form. Type `c q x` and Enter. The results must stay. Move the focus to Close and hold Enter: the results must stay.
14b. In a new match press Esc, Game, Leave game, move the focus to Forfeit and hold Enter. The results come and stay (the page does not leave).
15. A room, two browsers: the leader on the room's screen opens the gear and types `s`, `t`, a word and Enter. The countdown must not start and no chat line is sent. Then the guest opens the gear and the leader presses START: the guest's form stays over the countdown, closes by itself when it ends, the guest's next press on the gear opens the full menu, and the guest's keys work in the match.
16. A phone: tap the gear on the setup screen. Fullscreen and Picture shape show (no mouse row). A tap on the dimmed picture over START does nothing. Tap Close.
17. Click the gear and Enter fullscreen: the form is inside the fullscreen picture. Close it: there is no gear. Leave fullscreen: the gear is back.
