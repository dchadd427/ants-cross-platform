# The computer players in 187: the brawl

How a standard bot plays the game type 187 ([GAMEPLAY.md](GAMEPLAY.md#187-a-game-type-of-the-remake): no food and no power-up, the kills are the score, the last side with ants wins). The bots in general are in [BOTS.md](BOTS.md).

A standard bot of any level plays **`BrawlTask`** in 187 instead of every task of [BOTS.md](BOTS.md#the-standard-bot). `StandardBot::think` reads the room's mode from the engine it plays in (`BotView::game_mode()`; every client of a room knows it) and hands the whole look to the brawl. The profile (look interval, reaction time, command budget), the accept rule for an invitation and the rules of fairness are the level's as always; a match of the original's game never reaches the code (AI30.3: the same orders to the tick with the switch on and off; `bot_arena` reports of the original's game are byte for byte those of before). `LevelPlan::brawl187` is the switch, and `bot_arena --tune brawl=0` is the bot before: it plays the food game's tasks in 187 too, which have no food to work on (it strikes back at a blow and hunts what is hurt, and nothing else). There is no power-up in 187, so there is none to take.

## What the engine allows

Measured with the bot's own orders and in hand-made worlds; the times are Hard's (looks 4 ticks apart, orders leave 8 ticks after the look).

- **One blow lands per hit clip**: about every 40 ticks on one victim, however many ants strike it. A worker's blow takes 1 hit point and throws the victim one tile away from the attacker, a Combat Ant's punch takes 2 and throws it four. The victim stays in its hit clip for about 15 ticks after it landed, and an attacker that reaches a victim in its clip is refused ("can't do that") and stops.
- **An attack order names a tile**: the ant that stands there when the order arrives is the target. A victim that was struck lands on another tile, so the next blow needs an order that names the new tile, and an ant that walks has moved on before the attacker arrives: it is not caught by an order.
- **An ant left with one hit point walks home** at the speed of its hunters ([BOTS.md](BOTS.md#fights-of-its-own-health-the-kill-and-the-skirmish-contest-batch)) and is caught only when a hunter is beside it as its walk begins, or when its way is blocked. In a lab of one hunter and one victim with two hit points the second blow killed it from two of the eight sides, from different sides at different places of the map: no side works everywhere.
- **Water kills whatever the hit points**: a victim thrown into water drowns, onto a bomb it loses 2, onto a fire wall 1. In 412 deaths of two Hard brawl bots on 45 maps, 40 percent were drownings, 42 percent the last hit point of a plain ant, 9 percent Combat punches (maps whose ants are Combat Ants) and 9 percent bombs and fire of the map.
- **222 of the 540 maps of the library (41 percent) join the hills of seats 0 and 1 by no walk** (walls of fire and bombs, water, islands). On most of them nobody can be struck and a match ends at the clock without a kill.

## What the brawl does

At every look:

1. An ant with 3 hit points or fewer (`retreat_hp`) goes home to heal (the hill restores all hit points) and fights again with 9 or more.
2. The enemy ants that can be struck (`attackable`: not an ally's, none on a hill tile or in water) and that some ant of the bot can reach by a walk (the walker components of the start; on a level of Swimmers or Fire Ants the question is not asked) are ranked: the wounded first (a hit point counts three tiles), then the near. An enemy that walks is only an object once it is within two tiles of an ant.
3. Each victim gets a **pack** of the nearest ready ants, at most 2 (`per_target`); ants without a victim of their own join the nearest fight, 3 on a victim at most (`crowd`). Blows land in parallel on different victims, and the third ant finishes. A pair that was refused twice within 160 ticks ("can't go" with no hit clip of the victim to explain it) rests 300 ticks, so a fenced-in enemy draws a few orders and not a flood.
4. A pack is ordered onto its victim at once when an ant joined, and again (no sooner than 8 ticks later) when the victim was thrown to another tile or an ant of the pack stands idle after its blow; one command names the whole pack. A victim with one hit point that walks home is followed only by ants within two tiles of it.
5. When no enemy ant can be struck (every one is in its hill) the idle ants march to the door of the nearest enemy hill that a walk reaches, never an ally's, and wait there. When enemy ants are in sight but none can be reached, nobody moves.

## Measured

`bot_arena --game-mode 187`, Hard unless said, `standard+styled=1,brawl=1` (the brawl) against `standard+styled=1,brawl=0` (the bot before), every pair played in both seatings, seeds 1 and 2 for Hard and 1 for the others. The 540 maps of the library are split in two halves of 270: the **design** half (the maps in the even places of the list: the six originals, then the community maps by file name), on which the bot was built, and the **held-out** half (the odd places), which was not looked at until the end. A map is **joined** when a walk joins the hills of seats 0 and 1 (161 of the design half, 157 of the held-out half). The share is wins plus half the draws.

## Tried and left out

Hard, 90 maps of the design half, seeds 1 and 2, the variant against the bot as it ships; 50 is a tie and one standard error is about 2.2.

- **Walking an ant to the side of a victim from which one blow kills it** (water, a bomb or a fire wall behind the victim) before it strikes: the bot with the walk scored 42.5 against the bot without it, the bot that ships. The victims that drown anyway are the ones the packs hit from the right side by themselves, and the walk takes the ant out of its pack. A bonus for victims with water beside them, without the walk, scored 54.2 and 55.0 against the bot with the walk, where the plain bot scored 57.5.
- **Striking from the tile on the victim's way home** (so that a retreat leads back past the striker), in an earlier form of the code: 28.6.
- **Packs of 1, 3 and 4; crowds of 2, 4 and 5; retreat at 1, 2, 4 and 5 hit points; orders again after 4 and 14 ticks; the victims near the own hill first; victims within 12 or 20 tiles only; the hit points counted at 10 and at 60:** all between 46.4 and 52.2.
- **A door camp** (ants on the ring tiles of the enemy hill kill what comes home wounded): in a lab of one wounded enemy and 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8 ants on the ring it was killed in 5, 5, 5, 8, 10 and 0 of 15 tries; not built.

## Limits

- A map that no walk joins is not fought on (see above), and a match on it ends at the clock.
- The 16 maps whose ants are all Bombers could plant bombs; the brawl does not.
- A hurt ant walks home alone and is hunted by whoever stands in its way; it does not wait for an escort.
- Two allied brawl bots do not help each other beyond not striking each other.
- The opening is the same on every map: every ant goes for the nearest enemy ant it can reach.
