# Public rooms and the list of games (network protocol 22)

The leader of a lobby room ([`NETWORK_PORT.md`](NETWORK_PORT.md#protocol-16-the-lobby-room-the-front-page-waits-in-v0120)) can open it to everybody, and the game server lists the rooms that a newcomer can join in `GET /lobbies` of its public door. A new room is private. A private room is not hidden: it is listed as a locked card that nobody can join from, and its code and link never leave the room (see "Private rooms in the list"). The front page sends the message (the Room's Public | Private switch and Quick host) and shows the list (docked in Home's body, always shown): [`HOME_SCREEN.md`](HOME_GAMES.md#games-quick-join-quick-host-and-the-public-room). Public rooms change no rule of the simulation, golden hash or lock-step rule. The same number also holds one rule fix that only a map with the layer-2 id 38 shows ([`REPLAYS.md`](REPLAYS.md#the-file)).

## The protocol

* `kProtocolVersion` goes from 21 to **22**: one message and one Room flag are new. A Hello of 21 is refused with `VersionMismatch`, and so is a Hello of 22 by a server of 21 (the Hello's layout is unchanged). Recordings play on their rules number ([`REPLAYS.md`](REPLAYS.md#the-file)), so none is stranded; the restart records of 21 are refused for their protocol, as with every bump.
* **Visibility** (type 35, client to server, `MsgType::Last`): `[35][public u8]`, two bytes, 0 private or 1 public; nothing else is a Visibility. It is heard under a Plan's conditions: from the leader of an open lobby room whose START does not wait. Any other is ignored and counted (`ignored_visibility`; free up to 16 per connection, then a violation each; a minute forgives one), and in a room that is no lobby room it is garbage. Budget (`flood.hpp`): a burst of 3, then 1 a second; 6 more are tolerated and dropped, each one after that is a violation. The budget is the Visibility's own: it uses none of a Plan's.
* **Room**: the flags have **`kRoomPublic`** (8), only with `kRoomLobby` (the decoder refuses it otherwise). A desktop game that joins a public lobby decodes the flag and shows nothing of it.
* **The choice is the leader's.** A color move, a held seat and a newcomer leave it, but **another person's lead ends it**: when the leader left or was thrown out, the room is private and the new leader chooses for itself. The lead that goes with a person to another color is the same person (`HostLobby::elect_leader` compares the person's join order, not the seat). A lobby that the server opens again after a match keeps the choice: a room that was public is public again, for the person who made it so (`Rematch::public_by`), who leads it again when back; while a seat is kept it is not listed, and if another person of the match leads first the choice waits for the one who made it (it ends if that person is removed or gone). A private room's rematch is private.
* **The idle rule.** A public room in which nothing has been done for **30 minutes** (`HostLobby::activity()`: a join, a color move, a changed plan or name, a removal, a line of chat, a START, a Visibility that was done; not a ping, nor a link that comes back) becomes private by itself, and everybody is told. "Public" said to a public room shows nothing and counts as use, so a page keeps its room public by repeating it (the front page does, every ten minutes, while its host looks at the Room on a visible tab: the repeat is also a free heartbeat for the pool of [`SERVER.md`](SERVER.md#lobby-rooms)). "Private" said to a private room does nothing and is not use. The age of a listing is capped at 30 days (`kLobbyListedAgeCapMs`), so the 32-bit clock cannot make a room that has been public for 49.7 days look young.

## The list

`GET /lobbies` of the public door (`--replay-port`; the site's nginx passes it: "Replays" in [`SERVER_REPLAYS.md`](SERVER_REPLAYS.md#replays)): read only, no secret, GET only. A query is a 404 (an empty `?` answers as for `/live`); the real listener answers another method with 405 before the handler. It does not need the replay store (a server without replays answers it; one without lobby rooms answers an empty list). The answer is `Cache-Control: no-store` JSON:

```json
{"format":1,"lobbies":[{"code":"k7m2xq","host":"Ada","map":"TINY.LVL","mode":"highest-score","people":2,"bots":1,"free":1,"size":4,"age":95},{"private":true,"host":"Bea","map":"SMALL.LVL","mode":"187","people":1,"bots":0,"free":3,"size":4}],"count":1}
```

The first row is a public room, the second a private one (below). `count` is the number of public rows.

* **Listed** (`Room::listing`) while the room is public, waits (no match loads or runs), its START does not wait, a person's connection is open (a room that only holds seats is out within a minute), a color is free for a newcomer and no seat is kept for a person of its last match.
* **Order** (`RoomManager::lobbies`, a total order): more people first, then the longer public, then the smaller code; at most **50**, the best 50. `count` is the number of public rows listed. The rows of private rooms come after them (below).
* **A row** has these nine fields and nothing else: `code`, `host` (the leader's name, also while the leader's seat is held; a leader who gave no name is "Player" and its color's number), `map` (the file), `mode` (`"highest-score"` or `"187"`), `people` (persons; a held seat counts), `bots` (colors that nobody holds and the plan gives to a bot), `free` (colors that a newcomer takes), `size` (colors in the match: `people + bots + free`; a person on a color that the plan calls Nobody still plays, so the color counts) and `age` (seconds public).
* **Never** an address, a key, a platform, another person's name, plan letters or chat: the test of the answer searches the whole text for the keys' hex and the pages' addresses. A private row never has a code either (next section).
* **The list is bounded.** It holds the best 50 rows, people first. Names are text as typed (printable ASCII, up to 32 characters, `< > " \` among them): a page shows them as text (`textContent`), as for `/live`.
* **The status** of a lobby room (`GET /rooms/<code>`, the list of rooms, the result file) has `"public"`, the leader's choice.

## Private rooms in the list

A lobby room that its leader did not make public is listed too, as a **locked card**: the front page draws it with a padlock and a PRIVATE tag, with no Join button, no link and nothing to press, and Quick join never tries it. Private still means that the room is joined by invitation only: nothing in the list leads to the room.

* **Listed** (`Room::private_listing`) under every condition of a public row except the leader's choice: the room waits, its START does not wait, a person is connected, a color is free and no seat is kept. A room that starts, loads or runs leaves the list as a public one does. The match of a private room stays watchable as before (`GET /live`, with the 30-second delay): private controls who can join, nothing else.
* **A row** is `{"private":true,"host","map","mode","people","bots","free","size"}` and nothing else: the fields of a public row without `code` and `age`. It is made from `PrivateListing`, a type that has no member for a code, a key or an id. `count` stays the public rows, and the rows of private rooms follow them in the same `lobbies` array. A reader that needs a code (the front page's Join, a client that opens a room) skips a row that has none; the page also drops a row that has a `code` of any value.
* **Order** (`RoomManager::private_lobbies`): more people first, then the map, the host and the numbers, a total order of the fields that the row shows and of nothing else. The pass over the rooms goes by code, so the order must teach nothing about a code. At most **50** rows, beside the 50 public ones.
* **Never** the room's code, link or key, in any spelling (as it is, in capitals, percent-encoded, in groups of three), and no id of any kind that leads to the room. The answer is the same for every visitor and the site's cache keeps one copy of it for two seconds, so nothing in it is about the visitor. The page tells the visitor's own room by what the row shows (host, map, seats) against the room it is in, and tags it Yours.
* **The page** draws at most six locked cards after the open rooms and then one line, "and N more private rooms". The heading says "3 open, 3 private, 2 in progress" ("none open, 2 private" when nothing can be joined, with a slim strip and Host a game in place of the empty card). The count on the Games button stays the joinable rooms. [`HOME_GAMES.md`](HOME_GAMES.md#games-quick-join-quick-host-and-the-public-room) has the rest.

## Tests

* The codec: `test_lockstep` N2.113 (the message, every value, every prefix, an extra byte, the type byte, every Room flag byte), N2.111 and `tests/data/lobby_messages.txt` (the bytes both ways, with the page's `web_lobby_net_check.js`), N2.3 (the fuzz draws every type byte and holds a public lobby Room among its seeds).
* The lobby: `test_lobby` N4.50 - N4.54 and N4.49d (a public room's rematch; who is heard; a leader who goes or is thrown out takes the choice with it; a lead that goes with a person to another color, a held seat and a key do not; the Visibility's budget and its forgiveness; the idle rule to the step, across the 32-bit clock; the colors of a listing by hand and by a random walk; the Hello of 21) and the rematch's lead in `test_lobby_rematch.cpp`.
* The list and the door: `test_server` S3.232 - S3.242 (shape and numbers, each filter, the order, the cap, no key or address, the door's methods and paths, no replay store, the idle rule on a real server, the status, the rematch's kept seats, a leader who leaves, a host with no name) and S3.247 - S3.249 for private rooms (a locked row's keys and numbers, the moves between the two kinds, each filter, no code or key in any spelling over a random walk of rooms, the order by the row's fields, the two caps).
* The page and the real program: `web_home_games_check.js` (a locked row and its dropped look-alikes, the card with nothing to press, the count line, the strip, the line after six cards, Yours, a page sweep for any code or address), `web_home_games_quickjoin_check.js` (Quick join never counts or tries a locked room), `web_hub_here_check.js` (a row with no code never makes a room public), `web_lobby_client_check.js` (the page's pace, replayed through the server's own bucket), `web_lobby_net_check.js`, `web_lobby_server_check.js` (the real client against a real server; the front page's host makes a room public and a second page joins it from the real list) and the demo part of `tests/scripts/test_ants_server.sh`.
* Mutants: `tests/data/public_rooms_mutants.json` (`python3 tools/mutate.py tests/data/public_rooms_mutants.json --keep-going --jobs 4`; every mutant is caught, among them a private row with a code, with the host's key, in code order or counted in `count`), `tests/data/home_games_mutants.json` for the page (a locked card with a Join, a link or a code, Quick join that takes a locked room), the page's own on a copy in `tests/scripts/test_web_lobby_net.py`.
