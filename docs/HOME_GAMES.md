# Home's games: the Games list, Quick join, Quick host and the public Room

Part of [Home and the Room](HOME_SCREEN.md), the front page's two screens. The chat dock, emoji and voice and the login are in [Home's chat](HOME_CHAT.md).

## Games, Quick join, Quick host and the public Room

`games.js` and `games.css` come after the first paint (the page appends them to the head; a script that does not come leaves the buttons disabled and the page as it was) and own everything about public rooms. The page's `client`, model and session stay the page's own; the script reads them through the small bridge that `lobby.html` hands to `home.js` (`client()`, `join`, `typed`, `name`, `handing()`, `cancel`, `hold`, with `info`, `make`, `leave`, `how`, `stalled`, `holds`).

- **Notices.** The notices of this part (the public room, "Your match is running.", "Joined Pebble's room.") are one **toast**: a box over the page, `position: fixed`, in the middle of the window and just above whatever lies at the bottom of the screen: the chat (on a phone's Chat view, just above the chat's field, so the field stays free), the strip of views of a phone, or, on the Room of a window up to 700 px wide, the bar with START! (START! stays free at every size). It is never put into Home's bar at the top. Where there is no room for it between that bar and what lies at the bottom (a phone held sideways, a window under about 400 px high), it covers the top of the chat's lines; on the Room, START! comes first, so there it can cover Copy link, the top of the card or the Room's slim bar until OK or the 6 seconds clear it. It is placed again when the window changes, when the screen changes (Home, Room), when a phone's view changes, when the chat folds or unfolds and when one of the page's strips above the bar comes or goes. It takes no room: the bar, the list, the players and the chat keep their size and place when it comes and goes. One toast at a time: a later notice replaces the one that is there. It has the page's colors (a gold edge for a warning), an **OK** button and, where the notice asks something (**Rejoin**, **Make it public**, **Reload**), that button too. A notice that asks nothing goes by itself after 6 seconds, and waits while the pointer or the focus is on it; one that asks stays until it is answered, replaced or closed. **OK** closes it, and so does Escape while the focus is on the toast (Escape pressed anywhere else, in the chat's field or to close the list or a question, leaves a toast that asks something where it is; the focus goes back to where it was). It comes in with a short fade and slide (none for a reader who asks for no motion), has `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"`, and its words are set as text. The sentences are the same as before. The page's own strips above the bar (the Rejoin offer, "Back from the match", the page's banner) are not part of this and still stand in the page.
- **The Room's switch.** The host sees **Invite only** (locked) and **Public** (listed in Open games) in the card, and under them a line that says what the choice does (for an invite-only room: "Others see it in Open games, locked, with your name and map. People join only with your link."); a guest reads "Public room" or "Invite-only room". A press sends one `Visibility` through `LobbyClient.setPublic` (the page's pace applies: a request that is held back sends nothing and says "One moment, then press again."); the switch shows what the last Room message said and never what a press asked. A room that went invite only after 30 minutes says so to its host with a one-tap **Make it public**; another person's lead says "This room is invite only again because the host changed." While the Room screen shows, the tab is visible and the host looks at a public room, the page says "public" again every 10 minutes (a refresh that counts as use; never from a hidden tab, from Home or by a guest, and not once the host has touched nothing (no pointer, key or touch) for 20 minutes: the room is then left to the server's 30-minute idle rule, and a touch brings the repeat back). A "One moment, then press again." goes by itself when the moment is over, and an Invite only that the server did not answer for 10 seconds is not taken for the leader's own choice when the room goes invite only by itself later. Each button is one line of text, the small word on the baseline of the big one, which the browser centers in the button (a flex row with `align-items: baseline` hangs at the top of a tall button): the middle of the capitals is within 1 px of the button's middle. Under 360 px the whole label scales with the window (`font-size: 4.7vw`, the small word at .765 em, 4 px of side padding), so that the small word of each button ("locked", "listed") stays inside it at every width from 300 px.
- **Quick host** flips the open room that you lead to Public and shows the Room ("Your room is public now. It shows in Open games; ..."); with no room it asks first, in a card over Home ("Host a game": **Public room**, which has the focus, **Invite only** and **Cancel**; each button 48 px high, Escape, Cancel and a press on the dimmed page around the card change nothing and the focus goes back to Quick host): Public makes the room (the page's own machinery) and sends `Visibility(public)` once the Room message says that the room is there, Invite only makes the same room and never sends it (the room is in the list as a locked card, and people join it only with its link); with a room of its own it does not ask this; a public room says "Your room is already public."; as the leader of an invite-only room with people in it it asks first ("Make your room public? Sage is in it."); as a guest it asks first ("Leave Pebble's room and host your own?"), and only then leaves and makes one; while the page is on its way to a game (a match for one too) it does nothing but say "The game is opening. If it does not, reload this page." with a **Reload** (a navigation that is stopped leaves the page here), and so do Join and Join by code; START waiting or a match to go back to (the page's Rejoin offer is up) says "Your match is running." with **Rejoin**, and so do Quick join, Join and Join by code, which open nothing; a full server gives the page's own "The server is full right now" strip.
- **Quick join** finds the best open public room and moves the page into it. One look at the list ([`PUBLIC_ROOMS.md`](PUBLIC_ROOMS.md); the answer of less than a second ago is as good), the page's own room left out by its code, the rest **best first**: the fullest room that still has a free seat (the fewest free seats), then the oldest (the longest listed), and rooms that tie keep the door's order. It tries up to **three** rooms, one after the other, 0 to 300 ms apart (at random), with the page's probe: a second client that only joins, so the old room is let go of only when a new one welcomes the page. A room that is full, started or gone is a reason for the next one; another server version (the page's reload banner) and 15 s of silence end the tries. While it works the card **Finding a game…** stands over Home (four seats, a bar, "Looking through 5 open games for the best one.", and a **Cancel**, mid-card under the bar, that takes no press for 700 ms: a double press is not a Cancel; Cancel and Escape let the second client go, and no seat is left behind). The Welcome of a room shows the card **Joined Pebble's room** (the room as it is now, "Opening the room…", **Go to the room**), and the Room opens by itself 1.5 s later or at the press, with no strip. Invite-only rooms are never counted or tried (a Hello needs a code and a locked row has none). If nothing is open, or the three that were tried were filled meanwhile, the card says "No open games right now." (or "Games list is off right now"), adds "2 invite-only rooms are locked, so Quick join skips them." when there are such rooms (the page's own room is not counted: "1 invite-only room is locked, so Quick join skips it."), and offers **Host one!** (Quick host's machinery without its Public or Invite only question, because the card says that the room goes public: it flips the page's own room to public, or makes one) and **Not now**. If other people are in the page's room (a guest always counts: the host is there) it asks first, in the card of the other questions, about the best room (tagged BEST): "Leave your room?" "Sage is in your room. If you go, Sage becomes the host." **Leave and join** / **Stay here**. Like Quick host and Games it is off while a hand-off is stalled (the page's words are its title) and does nothing but say "The game is opening…" on a page that is on its way to a game, and "Your match is running." when a match waits. Every card is described by its first line (`aria-describedby`), and the Joined card, which closes by itself and leaves no strip, is also told by a line that only a screen reader reads ("Joined Pebble's room.", a polite status line in the body). A press on Quick join or Quick host while a Join of the list waits shows the list again, with the line and the Cancel of that Join, and the toast says "Joining Pebble's room… Wait for it, or cancel it in the list." (it goes when the Join ends). In a window under 351 px tall or 261 px wide (a phone on its side, a page zoomed to 200 %) the cards keep their look but use smaller gaps, a smaller shadow ant and, under 261 px, a smaller title and text, so that both buttons of every card stand in the window without a scroll; the other sizes are as drawn.
- **The Games list** is the popover under the button (the mode of a list that is given no dock; Home always docks it in its body, above, so the popover is the same code that the page does not ask for): `GET /lobbies` ([`PUBLIC_ROOMS.md`](PUBLIC_ROOMS.md)) as rows, best first (the map's picture, host, map, the seats as four colored dots and n of the size, the bots, **Join**; the first other row, the room that Quick join takes, is tagged **BEST** when there are two or more other rows; the heading says "5 waiting · updates live" while the looks go well), the page's own room, when it is Public and in the answer (recognized by its code, the same test that Quick join uses to leave it out), as the **first row**, tagged **Yours** (the style of BEST; the same text as the other rows, but no **Join** and nothing to press), counted by the Games button and the heading, never tagged BEST and never taken by Quick join (BEST is the first other row, and only when there are two or more other rows); "No open games right now." with a line that says rooms show up when their host makes them Public and one button, **Host a game**, which does what Quick host in the bar does (the same Public room or Invite only question) - only when the list has no row at all (the own room included), "Games list is off right now" when the door answers 404, 503 or something that is not a list, and **Have a code?**, a gold text button at the right of the heading (a button with `aria-expanded`, 44 px high): it folds the Join by code box open in the flow right under the heading (a field, **Join** and a close button; the focus goes to the field, Enter joins, Escape or the close button shuts it and the focus goes back to the button; a phone upright puts the count on its own line under the title). The field takes a code as shown or a pasted room link (the code after `room=`, also in a `?join=...&room=` link). A Join opens a second client that only joins; the page's own room is let go of **only when the new one welcomes the page**, so a refusal (full, started, gone, another version, no answer in 15 s) costs nothing and is said in plain words; a room with other people in it is asked about first. While a Join waits (up to 15 s) the list says "Joining Pebble's room…" with a **Cancel** that lets the second client go (it takes no press for 700 ms: it stands where the Join button stood, and the second click of a double click is not a Cancel); a Welcome that comes to a page that is on its way to a game is let go of at once. A press on the question card or the name card is not "a press elsewhere": the list stays open for the words that tell why a Join did not go.
- **Invite-only rooms** are in the list as **locked cards** ([`PUBLIC_ROOMS.md`](PUBLIC_ROOMS.md#private-rooms-in-the-list): rows of `GET /lobbies` that say `private: true` and have no code). They stand after the open rooms, in the answer's order, in the same grid: the map's square dimmed, the host's name and the map, a dashed card, an **INVITE ONLY** tag (and the tag **187** for that mode), the seats as dots and n of the size and the bots, and a **padlock** in a dashed box where Join stands. A locked card is not a button and not a link and has no focus stop, no `data-code` and no address; a press on it does nothing. A screen reader hears one sentence for it ("Invite-only room, cannot join. Maple's room, Small, 2/4 seats taken, no bots.") and not its pieces. The page's own invite-only room, found by its host, map, mode and seats against the Room message (a locked row has no code to match), is the first of them and has the tag **Yours** after INVITE ONLY (a look-alike room may be tagged by mistake, and a stale answer may show it plain for a few seconds: only a tag). At most **six** cards are drawn; after them one dashed line says "and 6 more invite-only rooms" ("and 1 more invite-only room"; "and 44+ more invite-only rooms" from 50 on). The heading counts the parts that exist: "3 open, 3 invite only, 2 in progress", "1 open, 2 invite only", "none open, 2 invite only" (the invite-only rooms include the page's own). The count beside **Games** stays the open games and the matches in progress, as before: a locked room is not a game to join. With invite-only rooms and nothing to join, the big "No open games right now." card gives way to a slim strip, "No open games to join right now." with the same second line and **Host a game**, over the locked cards; with no invite-only room and nothing open the big card is as it was. A row that carries a `code` is not drawn at all, and nothing but the eight fields is ever read from a locked row, so an address, an id or a link that an answer might hold is never in the page. An invite-only room's match is not changed by any of this: it is on `/live` and watchable like every match.
- **The picture of a row** (a square of 56 px, 52 on a phone and 48 in the card of a question) is the map's: an original's own (`front/preview_<word>.png`), or, for a map of the players, the library's, `/maps/previews/<file name without .lvl, percent-encoded>.webp`, the address that `AntsMapPicker.previewPath` (`web/front/map_picker.js`, the rule of the match pages) makes from the row's checked file name and that `GET /maps` gives as `thumb`, so no list of maps is fetched for it (`tests/scripts/web_home_games_check.js` draws all 534 maps of `Community-Maps/` and checks each address against `previews.json`). A picture is loaded when it is near (lazily). One that does not come in either ending (see "Pictures" in [PLAY_IN_BROWSER.md](PLAY_IN_BROWSER.md#choosing-a-map): a map that a player sent and the maintainer approved, and a test map of the Map Maker, have a `.png`, not a `.webp`), a failed load or a page without the picker, is taken away and the plain square stays: never a broken image.
- **Polling:** every 30 s with the popover closed, every 5 s while it is open (the docked list is always open: every 5 s while Home shows) and at once when it opens, +-20 % each, doubled after every failure up to 5 minutes (1 minute while open), never while the tab is hidden or the Room shows, and never two looks less than a second apart (a tab or a screen that comes and goes does not make a look each). The count on the button caps at "50+" and is hidden while the list is off. An answer that says it is over 2 MB is not read; rows are made again only when a room's host, map or seats changed (a press that straddles a look is not lost).
- **Games in progress.** The matches that run now stay in the list as rows under the open rooms (a box with the heading "In progress", there only while a match runs: with none the box is not shown at all and the list's head keeps its plain "N waiting" words), each with a red **Live** mark (and, for a match of mode 187, the tag **187** right after it, in the look of the history list's mode tag; a highest-score match has none, and neither has a match that the door sends with no mode), the map's square and name, who plays (a dot in each player's color, a ring for a computer player as in the open rooms, and the names as the door writes them: a server names its bots like "Pip (bot, Hard)" or "Kit (bot, idle)", an older one "Bot (Medium)"; all are rings), how long it has run ("2:05 so far"; **"Starting..."** for a match whose room has just started it and whose first turn has not run: a match is on the list from the moment its room starts it) and **Watch**. Watch is a link to `/play?live=<id>` ([`REPLAYS.md`](REPLAYS.md#the-match-pages)). While the page holds no room it opens in **this tab** (nothing is lost, and Back and Home come back to the one front page); while the page holds a room it opens in a **new tab** (`target="_blank"`, `rel="noopener"`), so that the seat stays, and that tab's Home closes it again instead of loading a second front page ([`SITE_NAV.md`](SITE_NAV.md#a-tab-that-the-front-page-opened), the one mechanism of Watch and of the Matches links). A press decides at that moment, whatever was drawn. For that the page answers a tab that asks on the channel `ants-watch` (for a match that the list showed while the page held a room, and for Matches once it has held a room; the answer carries nothing of the room; the page itself is never reloaded). There is no Join on a row in progress. They come from `GET /live` (every match from the moment its room starts it, the newest 50, all of them shown, a match of computer players too): that door carries no room code (an id holds the map and the second the match began, nothing of the room), and `GET /lobbies` lists a room only while it waits and has a free seat, so a room that fills or starts is no longer a row of the open list and its match is its own row here at once (`/live` lists a match from its start, and a look at it is brought forward when a room has left the open list). No room has to be matched to a match, and a match whose room the list never had is a row like any other. There is no switch: the matches always show, and nothing about them is kept in this browser (a word that an earlier version stored, `ants.home.games.live`, is never read and left as it is). The heading says "3 open, 2 in progress" while the rows are shown (the other words as before when the door is not there); the count beside **Games** (the phone's Games tab shows the same number) is the open games and the matches in progress together, so it is not 0 while matches run (it says "50+" from 50 on, as the list does; a screen reader hears "Games, 1 open, 2 in progress"), and with no match shown (none runs, or the door of the matches does not answer) it is the open games alone. It is one list: the open rooms first, then the box of matches directly under them. With no open room but matches in progress, there is no "No open games right now." card at all: the box of matches is the first thing under the heading, and the first match shows without a scroll. The empty card (that line, the line under it, the picture and the Host a game button) shows only when there is literally nothing to list: no open room and no match in progress. With none in progress the box says "No games in progress.". `/live` is looked at every **5 seconds at the least** (5 to 6 s, doubled after each failure up to 5 minutes; a return to Home or a tab that shows again waits out that backoff too), once when the list shows and never faster than 5 s after the last look, except that a look comes within 1.5 s of the last when a room that the list showed is gone from it (the room may have started its match, which is on `/live` from that moment: the row of the match appears within a few seconds of the room's row going), (a failed look and a list that only gained a room bring nothing forward), only while the list is drawn (a docked list: Home on screen with a visible tab, and on a phone not while the Chat or Players view covers it; a popover: while it is open), never while the tab is hidden or the Room shows, and never after START reaches the page. A server with no live door (404), a failure, a look that does not end or an answer that is no list hides the box and says nothing: no notice, no row; the open rooms go on. The door sends the map as a whole file name ("TREASURE.LVL", "DRY LAND.lvl"), which is checked by the same rule as the map of an open room and shown the same way. A name or a map is set as text and never as markup; the only address made from the answer is the player's, from an id of the server's shape (and encoded).
- **START** puts the list away, ends its toast and stops its looks, the way it takes the bars' presses (the toast of the list is inert with them), and the switch of the Room cannot be pressed. A hand-off that is still here after 15 seconds leaves the page's room frozen (see "START while a guest sits on Home"), so **Quick host**, **Quick join** and **Games** stay off, each with the page's words as its title, the switch says them under itself, and the list asks for nothing and polls nothing until the page is reloaded; a part that loads after START is put in the same state.
- **Untrusted text.** Every field of a row is checked (the nine fields of the answer, their types and ranges; a name is printable ASCII, a map a file name, a code lower case; a locked row's eight fields), a row that does not fit is dropped, at most 50 rows of each kind are read (the open rooms and the locked ones), and all of it is drawn with `textContent`: never markup, never a link.
