# Home and the Room: the front page's two screens

The front page (`web/lobby.html`, served at `/`) is one page with one WebSocket and two screens: **Home**, a top bar over the Yard (the live site's: the Games list), and the **Room**, the room page that [Play in the browser](PLAY_IN_BROWSER.md#your-room) describes, under a slim bar. `<body data-screen="home">` or `data-screen="room"` says which one shows. The page's client of the game server (`web/front/lobby_net.js`), its model and its session storage are the same for both, so going from one screen to the other is a class and one render: instant, no request, and the seat is never dropped. Both screens are drawn as the approved pictures draw them. Everything new is in `web/home/` (`home.js`, `home.css`, the chat dock's `chat.js`, `chat.css`, the Games list's `games.js`, `games.css` and the players list's `players.js`, `players.css`; the last three load after the first paint), because `web/front/` is at its size cap and the page at its line limit.

## Home

- **The top bar:** the logo, **Quick host**, **Quick join**, **My room** (with how many of the colors that are in the match play, "3/4", once there is a room), **Games** (with how many games are open), and at the right the login chip and the Menu button (the chip is hidden until the chat's hub is there and offers a login: [the login](#the-login) below). Quick host, Quick join and Games are lit by `games.js` when it has loaded (until then they are drawn and disabled), and nothing on the bar is a dead button. **Nothing on either bar is ever cut off or pushed past the window's edge, at any width from 320 px up** (`tests/scripts/web_home_games_browser.js` checks Home and the Room at every tenth width and at both sides of every limit). The bar's width budget, for the widest state (the counts "4/4" and "50+", the login chip with a long name): from 1280 px it is as drawn (1273 px at most); the chip shows its circle only below 1280 px (1075 px needed); a step below 1077 px shrinks the gaps, the padding and the type (890 px); below 892 px it is one low row (no icons, "Room" for My room and no count on it: 773 px; a phone held sideways, 640 px wide and more, has it too); below 775 px the type shrinks once more (620 px, to 640 px wide); under 640 px it is the phone's three rows (184 px high), for a phone upright and for any window of that width, except that a phone held sideways (landscape, at most 500 px high) 360 to 639 px wide has **two compact rows** (106 px high, so the Yard keeps 206 px of a 568 x 320 window): Quick host and Quick join with the logo and the Menu on the first, My room (with its count), Games (with its count) and the login chip on the second (347 px needed for the widest state; the step starts at 360 px, and a window narrower than that keeps the three rows). **Players (N)** is on the bar only where the bar has three rows (a phone upright, or a window under 640 px: the first row, after the logo, [the players list](#the-players-list) below); in every other window it is a button in the Yard's corner, so none of the limits above moved for it (the real-browser check of the bar runs with the list there too). The name chip is in the budget above.
- **The page's height:** Home is exactly as tall as the window shows (`100dvh`, with `100vh` for old browsers, in `home.css`), less `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` as padding: a phone whose page reaches under the system bar (`viewport-fit=cover`) would otherwise put the bottom of the Yard and the chat strip under it. The bar, the Yard and the strip always lie inside the visible window (`web_home_games_browser.js`, part 1i).
- **My room** makes a private room the first time it is pressed (a new code, the first color, the remembered or a picked name) and shows it; every later press only shows it. **A plain visit makes no room.** A tab that holds a seat takes it back in the background, so My room stays live.
- **The Menu** (also in the Room's bar): Matches, Live now, Map maker, How it works, the Discord server, then the small links of the footer (Sprites and sounds, Changelog, GitHub, Feedback, Legal), the live numbers and the version. It is at most as tall as the window leaves under the bar and scrolls inside itself (a phone held sideways is about 360 px high). The footer is not drawn on Home. **How it works** is a dialog like the map list: while it is open the page behind it (both bars, the Room, the strips, the footer, the chat dock and the list's notice) takes no key or tap.
- **The Yard** (`#yard`, where the real Yard will mount) is the area under the bar. On the **live site**, which is built with no site label, it is a plain sandy field (the page's own sand colors) with **the Games list docked in it**: the same list as the popover below (one code: `games.js` paints the rows, polls `GET /lobbies` and runs Join, Join by code, Quick join), always shown, as a panel that scrolls inside itself, **centered in the window from 1280 px wide** (the same room at both sides, the chat panel's and a gap, 428 px: the list is 560 px wide from 1440 px and narrows to 400 px at 1280 px, so its middle is the window's and it never reaches the chat panel, open or folded; `tests/scripts/web_home_games_browser.js` measures it, opt-in), right of the chat panel in a window of 761 to 1279 px (a centered list would lie on the panel, which takes 404 px of the width: at 1024 px the list is 560 px wide and its middle is 208 px right of the window's) and above the chat strip on a phone (so no Join is covered; 721 to 760 px wide it stands above the chat panel). Press **Games** there and the focus goes to the list (there is no second copy); its count is the same. On the **test site** (the staging stack, whose web image is built with `ANTS_SITE_LABEL=staging`) Home is as it was: the Yard shows a **stand-in** (CSS and two small pictures drawn as data: food, rocks, three of your ants and two of other colors, a dashed edge, a label that prints the size that the Yard will have) and the Games list is the popover. Home finds out the way the page's title and footer do: the Dockerfile's step puts the label in the footer (`<strong id="site-label">`), and `home.js` builds the stand-in when that element is there and docks the list when it is not, so there is no second setting and no host name in the script; a build with no label (production, `build_web.sh`, a local build) is the live site's Home. The Yard's box is the same either way, so the bar, the chat panel and the sideways-phone rules do not change. Why: the stand-in is for trying the layout, and a visitor to the live site would take it for a missing feature; a lobby that lists the open games is the page they came for.
- **The strips** (the notices, the Rejoin button, "Back from the match") stand above both screens; Home is as tall as the window, strips included, so it never scrolls.

## The players list

`players.js` and `players.css` load after the chat dock, once, when the dock is mounted (`home.js` asks for them with the loader of the Games list; a page whose dock never comes has no list), and speak through the dock's socket: the page keeps one socket to the hub, and the list adds no address of its own. Nothing is kept in the browser. It is the hub's list of who has the front page open ([`HUB.md`](HUB.md#presence): the names are the chat's, a guest is counted and never named, nothing is stored); this page only draws it.

- **What it shows.** A header, **Players**, with the count (everybody: the visitor and the guests too, so it is the number that the chat's "here now" says, "500+" over 500; one person alone is no number; the buttons say "99+" over 99, because the bar of a phone has no room for more, and their names say the number), then the visitor first as **you** (the gold frame, the first letter in a circle, where the page itself says they are, not where the hub last heard; the visitor's row in the hub's list is the one that the welcome names, `me`, and is left out of the groups, so another person with the same name is not), a divider, and the groups **Moderators**, **Looking**, **Waiting** and **Playing** with their counts, each person by name (a moderator marked **mod**, and told where under the name, as the visitor is), and last **+ N guests, not logged in**. Under a name: nothing for a person who looks; a room that waits by its map and the colors that play, "Treasure . 2 of 4", or "Private room"; a match by its map and its players, "Islands . 3 players", or "Private game". A map's name is the page's own for an original map, and for a map of the players only where the Games list shows that room (its code) under the same name; otherwise the line has the seats and no name, because what a person says about a room is text from a stranger. A dot in the circle has the color of the group. Where a public room waits **and the Games list has that room** (its code) there is a **Join**: the Games list's own Join of that room is pressed (its questions, its notices, its refusals; a list that is a popover, as on the test site, is opened for it; a room that the list does not hold says "That game is not open any more."), and none on the visitor's own room. Past 200 named people the list ends with "and N more". Every name, map and number is checked as text from strangers, as the chat's lines are, and drawn with `textContent`.
- **Where.** From **1180 x 501 px**: a panel **260 px** wide (280 from 1600 x 900) at the right of the Yard, which its minus button folds to its header; the Games list that the live site docks in the Yard ends 14 px before it up to 1279 px wide (`players.css`: the list's own `right` and `width` plus the panel's), and from 1280 px it is the list's own rule that holds (it is centered in the window and ends well before the panel). A **phone upright, or any window under 640 px**: the button **Players (N)** in the bar's first row (the picture, the word and the number from 411 px, the word and the number from 381 px, the picture and the number below that, a little closer together under 360 px; under 341 px the logo is 76 px wide; with "99+", the widest label, nothing of the bar is cut off from 320 to 639 px), and a sheet over the bottom of the page (12 px over the system bar: `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`). **Any other window** (a tablet, a small desktop window, a phone held sideways): the button in the Yard's top-right corner (just the picture and the number in a window under 640 px wide) and a drawer at the right, 320 px wide; the Games list moves out of its way (under it, or narrower, in a window that is short). The sheet and the drawer are dialogs over a scrim (the focus goes to Close, Tab stays inside, Escape, Close and a press on the scrim close them and the focus goes back to the button; they are over the Menu and the Games popover); the panel's own fold is the minus button, and Escape inside it folds it. A sheet that is open when the Room shows, or when the window grows wide enough to dock the panel, is closed. The panel, the button and the sheet are inert with the bars during the hand-off to the game and under a dialog of the page.
- **When there is nothing to show.** The list shows nothing until the hub has sent it (no flash of "just you"), and for a hub that has no list (an older one: its welcome does not say so) nothing at all, and Home is the same as without the two files (a real browser finds nothing but a few pixels at the edges of rounded buttons, which it draws either way). A hub that is not there (the chat says "Chat is offline") is **Players list is off right now**, with a grey dot instead of the count, in the panel and the sheet; the list comes back by itself with the chat. Nobody else here: "Just you here. Host a game!" and a **+ Host a game** that presses Quick host (off while Quick host is).
- **What the page tells the hub.** Only a visitor who is named says anything: where they are, from the page's own room (no room: looking; a room that has not started: waiting; a match, or a page that is on its way to one: playing), a public room's map and seats (and its code, if the visitor is the host: the Join is on the host's row), a private room's nothing, at most every 3 seconds, and again at once on a new socket. The list is asked for once for each socket. A guest asks and says nothing. The page looks at its room once a second, so the visitor's own row follows a change at once and the hub is told within 4 seconds.
- **What it cannot show.** The game page opens no socket to the hub (it never did: the front page's seat is handed to it on START), and the hub lists a person only while their socket is open, so a person whose tab went to the game is out of the list until they are back on the front page. "Playing" is therefore a person whose page has heard START and is on its way to the match (a hand-off that is slow, or has not finished), or still has the front page open in a room that started; a list that shows everybody in a match needs the game page, or the game server, to say so, and the hub to hold that word past a closed socket or the game page to open one to it: not done here.

## Games, Quick join, Quick host and the public Room

`games.js` and `games.css` come after the first paint (the page appends them to the head; a script that does not come leaves the buttons disabled and the page as it was) and own everything about public rooms. The page's `client`, model and session stay the page's own; the script reads them through the small bridge that `lobby.html` hands to `home.js` (`client()`, `join`, `typed`, `name`, `handing()`, `cancel`, `hold`, with `info`, `make`, `leave`, `how`, `stalled`).

- **Notices.** The notices of this part (the public room, "Your match is running.", "Joined Pebble's room.") are one strip with the page's own look: with the page's strips above the bar on Home, and under the Room's slim bar on the Room, as drawn.
- **The Room's switch.** The host sees **Private** (link only) and **Public** (listed in Open games) in the card, a guest reads "Public room" or "Private room". A press sends one `Visibility` through `LobbyClient.setPublic` (the page's pace applies: a request that is held back sends nothing and says "One moment, then press again."); the switch shows what the last Room message said and never what a press asked. A room that went private after 30 minutes says so to its host with a one-tap **Make it public**; another person's lead says "This room is private again because the host changed." While the Room screen shows, the tab is visible and the host looks at a public room, the page says "public" again every 10 minutes (a refresh that counts as use; never from a hidden tab, from Home or by a guest, and not once the host has touched nothing (no pointer, key or touch) for 20 minutes: the room is then left to the server's 30-minute idle rule, and a touch brings the repeat back). A "One moment, then press again." goes by itself when the moment is over, and a Private that the server did not answer for 10 seconds is not taken for the leader's own choice when the room goes private by itself later. Each button is one line of text, the small word on the baseline of the big one, which the browser centers in the button (a flex row with `align-items: baseline` hangs at the top of a tall button): the middle of the capitals is within 1 px of the button's middle. Under 360 px the whole label scales with the window (`font-size: 4.7vw`, the small word at .765 em, 4 px of side padding), so that "link only" stays inside its button at every width from 300 px.
- **Quick host** flips the open room that you lead to Public and shows the Room ("Your room is public now. It shows in Open games; ..."); with no room it makes one (My room's machinery) and sends `Visibility(public)` once the Room message says that the room is there; a public room says "Your room is already public."; as the leader of a private room with people in it it asks first ("Make your room public? Sage is in it."); as a guest it asks first ("Leave Pebble's room and host your own?"), and only then leaves and makes one; while the page is on its way to a game (a match for one too) it does nothing but say "The game is opening. If it does not, reload this page." with a **Reload** (a navigation that is stopped leaves the page here), and so do Join and Join by code; START waiting or a match to go back to (the page's Rejoin offer is up) says "Your match is running." with **Rejoin**, and so do Quick join, Join and Join by code, which open nothing; a full server gives the page's own "The server is full right now" strip.
- **Quick join** finds the best open public room and moves the page into it. One look at the list ([`PUBLIC_ROOMS.md`](PUBLIC_ROOMS.md); the answer of less than a second ago is as good), the page's own room left out by its code, the rest **best first**: the fullest room that still has a free seat (the fewest free seats), then the oldest (the longest listed), and rooms that tie keep the door's order. It tries up to **three** rooms, one after the other, 0 to 300 ms apart (at random), with the page's probe: a second client that only joins, so the old room is let go of only when a new one welcomes the page. A room that is full, started or gone is a reason for the next one; another server version (the page's reload banner) and 15 s of silence end the tries. While it works the card **Finding a game…** stands over Home (four seats, a bar, "Looking through 5 open games for the best one.", and a **Cancel**, mid-card under the bar, that takes no press for 700 ms: a double press is not a Cancel; Cancel and Escape let the second client go, and no seat is left behind). The Welcome of a room shows the card **Joined Pebble's room** (the room as it is now, "Opening the room…", **Go to the room**), and the Room opens by itself 1.5 s later or at the press, with no strip. If nothing is open, or the three that were tried were filled meanwhile, the card says "No open games right now." (or "Games list is off right now") and offers **Host one!** (Quick host's machinery: it flips the page's own room to public, or makes one) and **Not now**. If other people are in the page's room (a guest always counts: the host is there) it asks first, in the card of the other questions, about the best room (tagged BEST): "Leave your room?" "Sage is in your room. If you go, Sage becomes the host." **Leave and join** / **Stay here**. Like Quick host and Games it is off while a hand-off is stalled (the page's words are its title) and does nothing but say "The game is opening…" on a page that is on its way to a game, and "Your match is running." when a match waits. Every card is described by its first line (`aria-describedby`), and the Joined card, which closes by itself and leaves no strip, is also told by a line that only a screen reader reads ("Joined Pebble's room.", a polite status line in the body). A press on Quick join or Quick host while a Join of the list waits shows the list again, with the line and the Cancel of that Join, and the strip says "Joining Pebble's room… Wait for it, or cancel it in the list." (it goes when the Join ends). In a window under 351 px tall or 261 px wide (a phone on its side, a page zoomed to 200 %) the cards keep their look but use smaller gaps, a smaller shadow ant and, under 261 px, a smaller title and text, so that both buttons of every card stand in the window without a scroll; the other sizes are as drawn.
- **The Games list** is the popover under the button (on the test site; the live site docks the same list in the Yard, above): `GET /lobbies` ([`PUBLIC_ROOMS.md`](PUBLIC_ROOMS.md)) as rows, best first (the map's picture, host, map, the seats as four colored dots and n of the size, the bots, **Join**; the first other row, the room that Quick join takes, is tagged **BEST** when there are two or more other rows; the heading says "5 waiting · updates live" while the looks go well), the page's own room, when it is Public and in the answer (recognized by its code, the same test that Quick join uses to leave it out), as the **first row**, tagged **Yours** (the style of BEST; the same text as the other rows, but no **Join** and nothing to press), counted by the Games button and the heading, never tagged BEST and never taken by Quick join (BEST is the first other row, and only when there are two or more other rows); "No open games right now." with **Host one!** only when the list has no row at all (the own room included), "Games list is off right now" when the door answers 404, 503 or something that is not a list, and **Join by code** at the bottom (it replaces the Room card's "Have a code?"). A Join opens a second client that only joins; the page's own room is let go of **only when the new one welcomes the page**, so a refusal (full, started, gone, another version, no answer in 15 s) costs nothing and is said in plain words; a room with other people in it is asked about first. While a Join waits (up to 15 s) the list says "Joining Pebble's room…" with a **Cancel** that lets the second client go (it takes no press for 700 ms: it stands where the Join button stood, and the second click of a double click is not a Cancel); a Welcome that comes to a page that is on its way to a game is let go of at once. A press on the question card or the name card is not "a press elsewhere": the list stays open for the words that tell why a Join did not go.
- **The picture of a row** (a square of 56 px, 52 on a phone and 48 in the card of a question) is the map's: an original's own (`front/preview_<word>.png`), or, for a map of the players, the library's, `/maps/previews/<file name without .lvl, percent-encoded>.webp`, the address that `AntsMapPicker.previewPath` (`web/front/map_picker.js`, the rule of the match pages) makes from the row's checked file name and that `GET /maps` gives as `thumb`, so no list of maps is fetched for it (`tests/scripts/web_home_games_check.js` draws all 534 maps of `Community-Maps/` and checks each address against `previews.json`). A picture is loaded when it is near (lazily). One that does not come, a 404 (a map that the library has no picture of), a failed load or a page without the picker, is taken away and the plain square stays: never a broken image. A map that a player sent and the owner approved, and a test map of the Map Maker, have no `.webp`; the game server renders their `.png` for the picker's list, which this row does not ask for, so their square stays plain.
- **Polling:** every 30 s with the popover closed, every 5 s while it is open (the docked list is always open: every 5 s while Home shows) and at once when it opens, +-20 % each, doubled after every failure up to 5 minutes (1 minute while open), never while the tab is hidden or the Room shows, and never two looks less than a second apart (a tab or a screen that comes and goes does not make a look each). The count on the button caps at "50+" and is hidden while the list is off. An answer that says it is over 2 MB is not read; rows are made again only when a room's host, map or seats changed (a press that straddles a look is not lost).
- **START** puts the list away, ends its notice and stops its looks, the way it takes the bars' presses (the strip of the list is inert with them), and the switch of the Room cannot be pressed. A hand-off that is still here after 15 seconds leaves the page's room frozen (see "START while a guest sits on Home"), so **Quick host**, **Quick join** and **Games** stay off, each with the page's words as its title, the switch says them under itself, and the list asks for nothing and polls nothing until the page is reloaded; a part that loads after START is put in the same state.
- **Untrusted text.** Every field of a row is checked (the nine fields of the answer, their types and ranges; a name is printable ASCII, a map a file name, a code lower case), a row that does not fit is dropped, at most 50 rows are shown, and all of it is drawn with `textContent`: never markup, never a link.

## The Room

The room page as it was, with the logo row replaced by a slim bar: **Home** (the room stays open), the logo, **Leave room** (leaves it and shows Home; the next My room makes a new room; when other people are in the room it asks first, in the card of the other questions: "Leave your room?" with **Leave room** and **Stay here**, which has the focus, and for a guest "Leave Pebble's room?"; alone it leaves at once) and the Menu. The old header's buttons live in the Menu and are hidden. Below 340 px the picture of the logo is left out of the bar (the heading keeps its name).

## Addresses

| Address | Screen |
|---|---|
| `/` | Home |
| `/#room` | the Room (My room, or the Home button, pushes it: Back goes to Home, Forward back to the Room) |
| `/?room=CODE` | the Room, behind the name card (a link opens the Room directly) |
| `/#room` after a reload, in a tab that holds a seat | the Room again; in a tab with none, Home |

A reload keeps the screen. The page writes nothing to the history until a button is pressed; the entry that it opened on is marked with its screen the first time a button leaves it, with its address unchanged.

## START while a guest sits on Home

The hidden Room still takes the hand-off: the key of the seat is written for the game page (`ants.rejoin.<room>.<seat>`), the tab goes to the game page, and Home shows **Opening the game…** at once. The hand-off never looks at the screen, and from then on both bars take no press (a Leave room would take the seat out from under the game that is about to take it over). A hand-off that is still here 15 seconds later has failed, was stopped or is only slow: the card goes and Home takes presses again, but this page's room stays as it was (a page on its way takes no message), so the notice strip says "The game is slow to open. Reload this page to get your seat back." with a **Reload** button (the page that comes up offers **Rejoin it**), and "The game did not open. ..." once a minute has gone since START (a page that is only slow is not told that it failed), and **Leave room** stays off: a Leave would end the START for everybody and leave a game page that is only slow with no seat to take over (a game that comes late takes the seat with the key).

## The chat dock

Home carries the site's global chat in a panel in its lower-left corner: 380 x 380 on a desktop, which the minus button folds to its header; on a phone or a short window, a strip 62 px high along the bottom that a press anywhere on it opens upward (490 px high upright, 420 x 286 sideways). `chat.js` and `chat.css` load once, after the first paint, when Home is first shown (a link that opens the Room loads nothing until Home shows), and no other page loads them or opens a hub socket. The open panel keeps under Home's bar (under the two compact rows of a phone held sideways, 106 px, it is shorter: 202 px high at 568 x 320): notices above Home (and a small phone) take the room, and when Home has less than the panel's least height and 12 px under its bar (200 px on a phone, 150 sideways: `--gc-min` in `chat.css`) the strip shows instead of the panel, and nothing when not even the strip (62 px) fits; a press that wants the panel then says "No room to open it: close a notice" on the strip for a few seconds (and the button's name gives the reason); the panel comes back, as the visitor left it, when the room does. The socket stays open while the Room shows (and [the players list](#the-players-list) speaks through it: the dock hands it the welcome, the frames of the list and the loss of the link, and writes what the list sends), so the **unread count** on the strip and the header goes on: the lines that came while the dock was folded, on the Room or in a hidden tab, not the visitor's own and not the history.

- **The log** shows `name: text`, the moderators' lines marked "mod", 100 lines at most (`del` takes one away). All of it is text from strangers: made with `textContent` only, never markup or a link, cut to 32 and 200 characters, the invisible characters that the hub removes taken out here too (a test keeps the two lists in step; a name loses its variation selectors as well), each name and line in a `<bdi>`; a frame that is too big, not JSON or of a kind that is not known is ignored. The log is `role="log"`, polite, and a flood is not read out line by line: two lines in 3 s are, then one summary ("8 more messages"), which goes when the next window of 3 s starts.
- **Who may write** is the hub's welcome (`docs/HUB.md`). Without `you` and with `login:false` the dock is read-only: "Chat is read-only for now", no field and no Send. With `login:true` and no `you` the field is off and says "Log in with Discord to chat", and a Log in button is where Send would be (as drawn). With `you` the field and Send are there, and the dock's login place shows who it is ([the login](#the-login)). A line goes out only on an open, welcomed socket, at the page's own pace (two at once, then one every 2.5 s; the hub's is three and 2 s), and every `err` code has its plain words (a line with `<` or `>` is not sent: "Lines cannot hold < or >"; a text that the hub accepted in the last 31 s is not sent again, as the hub remembers every one; a text that it refused is forgotten here, so it may be sent again: the hub answers in order, so the refused line is the oldest that has no answer yet, whatever was said since; a line is answered by its echo, which the hub composes (NFC) and may take characters out of, so an echo that is the line composed, or the line with characters taken out, answers the oldest line that it is part of; a moderator's command (`/mute ...`) waits in the same list as the lines and has no answer when the hub takes it: the hub says `bad` to a wrong one, so a `bad` is the oldest command's and any other refusal the oldest line's (a command and a line sent together are told apart), and a command is never put back in the field, as Enter would issue it again; a send that nobody answers is forgotten after 8 seconds). "7 here now" says "500+" over the hub's 500.
- **Offline.** A hub that is not there, slow or garbage is "Chat is offline" and "reconnecting…": a try after 1 s, doubling to 60 s, each wait spread from half to one and a half times (never under 1 s or over 60 s), so that pages that lost one hub do not come back in waves; a link must hold 30 s to start again at 1 s; a tab or a network that comes back tries at once. A ping every 25 s, and a hub that does not answer for 30 s is let go. A hidden tab keeps the socket and paints nothing. Nothing is kept in the browser, and nothing the chat does can block Home, the Room or START.

## The login

The login is the hub's ([`HUB.md`](HUB.md#login): Discord, the one scope `identify`); the page only shows it, and never sees a token or a cookie (the cookie is `HttpOnly`). It is there only where the welcome says `login:true`, so a hub without a Discord application shows none of it.

- **Logged out** (`login:true`, no `you`): the chip in the bar (the mark of Discord and "Log in"; the mark alone on a phone, held sideways or in a window under 1280 px wide, 46 px) and the dock's button (the field is off, "Log in with Discord to chat"). Either one is a full navigation to `/hub/login?next=home` (`next=room` from the Room): the tab goes to Discord and comes back to the same screen, as drawn. The two ways back are `/` and `/#room`, chosen by the hub from that one word.
- **Logged in** (`you`): the chip is a circle with the first letter and the name (the circle alone on a phone or in a window under 1280 px wide), and a press opens the dock and puts the focus on Log out. In `#gchat-login-slot`: "Chatting as <name>", **Log out**, and a small menu (the dots) with **Forget me** and a sentence of what it does. Both are a `fetch` POST of this page to the hub (`/hub/logout`, `/hub/forget`: same origin, with its cookie, no redirect, one at a time); after either the dock drops its socket and connects again at once, as a reader. A POST that does not work says "That did not work, try again" and changes nothing. The name is text (cleaned and cut like a line's), and nothing of this is kept in the browser.
- **The round trip and a room.** The tab leaves for Discord and comes back: the session storage that holds the seat of the room (`ants.lobby`) belongs to the tab and survives it, so the page opens, takes the seat back in the background as after any reload, and shows Home (or the Room, for `next=room`; a link `?room=CODE` also works). The server holds the seat of a dropped page for its usual window, so a login that takes longer than that (a first visit that reads Discord's page for a minute) finds the seat gone, like any visitor who was away that long: the page then offers the room's usual Rejoin or makes no room, and Home is as it was. `tests/scripts/web_home_login_browser.js` presses it through in a real browser against a real game server.
