# Home and the Room: the front page's two screens

The front page (`web/lobby.html`, served at `/`) is one page with one WebSocket and two screens: **Home**, a top bar over a picture of the Yard, and the **Room**, the room page that [Play in the browser](PLAY_IN_BROWSER.md#your-room) describes, under a slim bar. `<body data-screen="home">` or `data-screen="room"` says which one shows. The page's client of the game server (`web/front/lobby_net.js`), its model and its session storage are the same for both, so going from one screen to the other is a class and one render: instant, no request, and the seat is never dropped. Both screens are drawn as the approved pictures draw them. Everything new is in `web/home/` (`home.js`, `home.css`, the chat dock's `chat.js`, `chat.css`, and the Games list's `games.js`, `games.css`; the last two load after the first paint), because `web/front/` is at its size cap and the page at its line limit.

## Home

- **The top bar:** the logo, **Quick host**, **Quick join**, **My room** (with how many of the colors that are in the match play, "3/4", once there is a room), **Games** (with how many games are open), and at the right the login chip and the Menu button (the chip is hidden until the chat's hub is there and offers a login: [the login](#the-login) below). Quick host, Quick join and Games are lit by `games.js` when it has loaded (until then they are drawn and disabled), and nothing on the bar is a dead button.
- **My room** makes a private room the first time it is pressed (a new code, the first color, the remembered or a picked name) and shows it; every later press only shows it. **A plain visit makes no room.** A tab that holds a seat takes it back in the background, so My room stays live.
- **The Menu** (also in the Room's bar): Matches, Live now, Map maker, How it works, the Discord server, then the small links of the footer (Sprites and sounds, Changelog, GitHub, Feedback, Legal), the live numbers and the version. It is at most as tall as the window leaves under the bar and scrolls inside itself (a phone held sideways is about 360 px high). The footer is not drawn on Home. **How it works** is a dialog like the map list: while it is open the page behind it (both bars, the Room, the strips, the footer, the chat dock and the list's notice) takes no key or tap.
- **The Yard** is a stand-in (CSS and two small pictures drawn as data): a sandy field with food, rocks, three of your ants and two of other colors, a dashed edge and a label that prints the size that the Yard will have. The real Yard mounts into `#yard`. Every target is at least 44 px high and wide, and there is no sideways scrollbar at 390 x 844, 844 x 390 and 1280 x 800.
- **The strips** (the notices, the Rejoin button, "Back from the match") stand above both screens; Home is as tall as the window, strips included, so it never scrolls.

## Games, Quick join, Quick host and the public Room

`games.js` and `games.css` come after the first paint (the page appends them to the head; a script that does not come leaves the buttons disabled and the page as it was) and own everything about public rooms. The page's `client`, model and session stay the page's own; the script reads them through the small bridge that `lobby.html` hands to `home.js` (`client()`, `join`, `typed`, `name`, `handing()`, `cancel`, `hold`, with `info`, `make`, `leave`, `how`, `stalled`).

- **Notices.** The notices of this part (the public room, "Your match is running.", "Joined Pebble's room.") are one strip with the page's own look: with the page's strips above the bar on Home, and under the Room's slim bar on the Room, as drawn.
- **The Room's switch.** The host sees **Private** (link only) and **Public** (listed in Open games) in the card, a guest reads "Public room" or "Private room". A press sends one `Visibility` through `LobbyClient.setPublic` (the page's pace applies: a request that is held back sends nothing and says "One moment, then press again."); the switch shows what the last Room message said and never what a press asked. A room that went private after 30 minutes says so to its host with a one-tap **Make it public**; another person's lead says "This room is private again because the host changed." While the Room screen shows, the tab is visible and the host looks at a public room, the page says "public" again every 10 minutes (a refresh that counts as use; never from a hidden tab, from Home or by a guest, and not once the host has touched nothing (no pointer, key or touch) for 20 minutes: the room is then left to the server's 30-minute idle rule, and a touch brings the repeat back). A "One moment, then press again." goes by itself when the moment is over, and a Private that the server did not answer for 10 seconds is not taken for the leader's own choice when the room goes private by itself later.
- **Quick host** flips the open room that you lead to Public and shows the Room ("Your room is public now. It shows in Open games; ..."); with no room it makes one (My room's machinery) and sends `Visibility(public)` once the Room message says that the room is there; a public room says "Your room is already public."; as the leader of a private room with people in it it asks first ("Make your room public? Sage is in it."); as a guest it asks first ("Leave Pebble's room and host your own?"), and only then leaves and makes one; while the page is on its way to a game (a match for one too) it does nothing but say "The game is opening. If it does not, reload this page." with a **Reload** (a navigation that is stopped leaves the page here), and so do Join and Join by code; START waiting or a match to go back to (the page's Rejoin offer is up) says "Your match is running." with **Rejoin**, and so do Quick join, Join and Join by code, which open nothing; a full server gives the page's own "The server is full right now" strip.
- **Quick join** finds the best open public room and moves the page into it. One look at the list ([`PUBLIC_ROOMS.md`](PUBLIC_ROOMS.md); the answer of less than a second ago is as good), the page's own room left out by its code, the rest **best first**: the fullest room that still has a free seat (the fewest free seats), then the oldest (the longest listed), and rooms that tie keep the door's order. It tries up to **three** rooms, one after the other, 0 to 300 ms apart (at random), with the page's probe: a second client that only joins, so the old room is let go of only when a new one welcomes the page. A room that is full, started or gone is a reason for the next one; another server version (the page's reload banner) and 15 s of silence end the tries. While it works the card **Finding a game…** stands over Home (four seats, a bar, "Looking through 5 open games for the best one.", and a **Cancel**, mid-card under the bar, that takes no press for 700 ms: a double press is not a Cancel; Cancel and Escape let the second client go, and no seat is left behind). The Welcome of a room shows the card **Joined Pebble's room** (the room as it is now, "Opening the room…", **Go to the room**), and the Room opens by itself 1.5 s later or at the press, with no strip. If nothing is open, or the three that were tried were filled meanwhile, the card says "No open games right now." (or "Games list is off right now") and offers **Host one!** (Quick host's machinery: it flips the page's own room to public, or makes one) and **Not now**. If other people are in the page's room (a guest always counts: the host is there) it asks first, in the card of the other questions, about the best room (tagged BEST): "Leave your room?" "Sage is in your room. If you go, Sage becomes the host." **Leave and join** / **Stay here**. Like Quick host and Games it is off while a hand-off is stalled (the page's words are its title) and does nothing but say "The game is opening…" on a page that is on its way to a game, and "Your match is running." when a match waits. Every card is described by its first line (`aria-describedby`), and the Joined card, which closes by itself and leaves no strip, is also told by a line that only a screen reader reads ("Joined Pebble's room.", a polite status line in the body). A press on Quick join or Quick host while a Join of the list waits shows the list again, with the line and the Cancel of that Join, and the strip says "Joining Pebble's room… Wait for it, or cancel it in the list." (it goes when the Join ends). In a window under 351 px tall or 261 px wide (a phone on its side, a page zoomed to 200 %) the cards keep their look but use smaller gaps, a smaller shadow ant and, under 261 px, a smaller title and text, so that both buttons of every card stand in the window without a scroll; the other sizes are as drawn.
- **The Games list** is the popover under the button: `GET /lobbies` ([`PUBLIC_ROOMS.md`](PUBLIC_ROOMS.md)) as rows, best first (host, map, the seats as four colored dots and n of the size, the bots, **Join**; the first row, the room that Quick join takes, is tagged **BEST** when there are two or more; the heading says "5 waiting · updates live" while the looks go well), the page's own room left out by its code, "No open games right now." with **Host one!** when nothing is open, "Games list is off right now" when the door answers 404, 503 or something that is not a list, and **Join by code** at the bottom (it replaces the Room card's "Have a code?"). A Join opens a second client that only joins; the page's own room is let go of **only when the new one welcomes the page**, so a refusal (full, started, gone, another version, no answer in 15 s) costs nothing and is said in plain words; a room with other people in it is asked about first. While a Join waits (up to 15 s) the list says "Joining Pebble's room…" with a **Cancel** that lets the second client go (it takes no press for 700 ms: it stands where the Join button stood, and the second click of a double click is not a Cancel); a Welcome that comes to a page that is on its way to a game is let go of at once. A press on the question card or the name card is not "a press elsewhere": the list stays open for the words that tell why a Join did not go.
- **Polling:** every 30 s with the popover closed, every 5 s while it is open and at once when it opens, +-20 % each, doubled after every failure up to 5 minutes (1 minute while open), never while the tab is hidden or the Room shows, and never two looks less than a second apart (a tab or a screen that comes and goes does not make a look each). The count on the button caps at "50+" and is hidden while the list is off. An answer that says it is over 2 MB is not read; rows are made again only when a room's host, map or seats changed (a press that straddles a look is not lost).
- **START** puts the list away, ends its notice and stops its looks, the way it takes the bars' presses (the strip of the list is inert with them), and the switch of the Room cannot be pressed. A hand-off that is still here after 15 seconds leaves the page's room frozen (see "START while a guest sits on Home"), so **Quick host**, **Quick join** and **Games** stay off, each with the page's words as its title, the switch says them under itself, and the list asks for nothing and polls nothing until the page is reloaded; a part that loads after START is put in the same state.
- **Untrusted text.** Every field of a row is checked (the nine fields of the answer, their types and ranges; a name is printable ASCII, a map a file name, a code lower case), a row that does not fit is dropped, at most 50 rows are shown, and all of it is drawn with `textContent`: never markup, never a link.

## The Room

The room page as it was, with the logo row replaced by a slim bar: **Home** (the room stays open), the logo, **Leave room** (leaves it and shows Home; the next My room makes a new room; when other people are in the room it asks first, in the card of the other questions: "Leave your room?" with **Leave room** and **Stay here**, which has the focus, and for a guest "Leave Pebble's room?"; alone it leaves at once) and the Menu. The old header's buttons live in the Menu and are hidden. Below 340 px the picture of the logo is left out of the bar (the heading keeps its name).

## Addresses

| Address | Screen |
|---|---|
| `/` | Home |
| `/#room` | the Room (My room, or the Home button, pushes it: Back goes to Home, Forward back to the Room) |
| `/?room=CODE` | the Room, behind the name card (a link opens the Room directly) |
| `/#room` after a reload, in a tab that holds a seat | the Room again; in a tab with none, Home |

A reload keeps the screen. The page writes nothing to the history until a button is pressed; the entry that it opened on is marked with its screen the first time a button leaves it, with its address unchanged.

## START while a guest sits on Home

The hidden Room still takes the hand-off: the key of the seat is written for the game page (`ants.rejoin.<room>.<seat>`), the tab goes to the game page, and Home shows **Opening the game…** at once. The hand-off never looks at the screen, and from then on both bars take no press (a Leave room would take the seat out from under the game that is about to take it over). A hand-off that is still here 15 seconds later has failed, was stopped or is only slow: the card goes and Home takes presses again, but this page's room stays as it was (a page on its way takes no message), so the notice strip says "The game is slow to open. Reload this page to get your seat back." with a **Reload** button (the page that comes up offers **Rejoin it**), and "The game did not open. ..." once a minute has gone since START (a page that is only slow is not told that it failed), and **Leave room** stays off: a Leave would end the START for everybody and leave a game page that is only slow with no seat to take over (a game that comes late takes the seat with the key).

## The chat dock

Home carries the site's global chat in a panel in its lower-left corner: 380 x 380 on a desktop, which the minus button folds to its header; on a phone or a short window, a strip 62 px high along the bottom that a press anywhere on it opens upward (490 px high upright, 420 x 286 sideways). `chat.js` and `chat.css` load once, after the first paint, when Home is first shown (a link that opens the Room loads nothing until Home shows), and no other page loads them or opens a hub socket. The open panel keeps under Home's bar: notices above Home (and a small phone) take the room, and when Home has less than the panel's least height and 12 px under its bar (200 px on a phone, 150 sideways: `--gc-min` in `chat.css`) the strip shows instead of the panel, and nothing when not even the strip (62 px) fits; a press that wants the panel then says "No room to open it: close a notice" on the strip for a few seconds (and the button's name gives the reason); the panel comes back, as the visitor left it, when the room does. The socket stays open while the Room shows, so the **unread count** on the strip and the header goes on: the lines that came while the dock was folded, on the Room or in a hidden tab, not the visitor's own and not the history.

- **The log** shows `name: text`, the moderators' lines marked "mod", 100 lines at most (`del` takes one away). All of it is text from strangers: made with `textContent` only, never markup or a link, cut to 32 and 200 characters, the invisible characters that the hub removes taken out here too (a test keeps the two lists in step; a name loses its variation selectors as well), each name and line in a `<bdi>`; a frame that is too big, not JSON or of a kind that is not known is ignored. The log is `role="log"`, polite, and a flood is not read out line by line: two lines in 3 s are, then one summary ("8 more messages"), which goes when the next window of 3 s starts.
- **Who may write** is the hub's welcome (`docs/HUB.md`). Without `you` and with `login:false` the dock is read-only: "Chat is read-only for now", no field and no Send. With `login:true` and no `you` the field is off and says "Log in with Discord to chat", and a Log in button is where Send would be (as drawn). With `you` the field and Send are there, and the dock's login place shows who it is ([the login](#the-login)). A line goes out only on an open, welcomed socket, at the page's own pace (two at once, then one every 2.5 s; the hub's is three and 2 s), and every `err` code has its plain words (a line with `<` or `>` is not sent: "Lines cannot hold < or >"; a text that the hub accepted in the last 31 s is not sent again, as the hub remembers every one; a text that it refused is forgotten here, so it may be sent again: the hub answers in order, so the refused line is the oldest that has no answer yet, whatever was said since; a line is answered by its echo, which the hub composes (NFC) and may take characters out of, so an echo that is the line composed, or the line with characters taken out, answers the oldest line that it is part of; a moderator's command (`/mute ...`) has no answer and waits for none; a line that nobody answers is forgotten after 8 seconds). "7 here now" says "500+" over the hub's 500.
- **Offline.** A hub that is not there, slow or garbage is "Chat is offline" and "reconnecting…": a try after 1 s, doubling to 60 s, each wait spread from half to one and a half times (never under 1 s or over 60 s), so that pages that lost one hub do not come back in waves; a link must hold 30 s to start again at 1 s; a tab or a network that comes back tries at once. A ping every 25 s, and a hub that does not answer for 30 s is let go. A hidden tab keeps the socket and paints nothing. Nothing is kept in the browser, and nothing the chat does can block Home, the Room or START.

## The login

The login is the hub's ([`HUB.md`](HUB.md#login): Discord, the one scope `identify`); the page only shows it, and never sees a token or a cookie (the cookie is `HttpOnly`). It is there only where the welcome says `login:true`, so a hub without a Discord application shows none of it.

- **Logged out** (`login:true`, no `you`): the chip in the bar (the mark of Discord and "Log in"; the mark alone on a phone or held sideways, 46 px) and the dock's button (the field is off, "Log in with Discord to chat"). Either one is a full navigation to `/hub/login?next=home` (`next=room` from the Room): the tab goes to Discord and comes back to the same screen, as drawn. The two ways back are `/` and `/#room`, chosen by the hub from that one word.
- **Logged in** (`you`): the chip is a circle with the first letter and the name (the circle alone on a phone), and a press opens the dock and puts the focus on Log out. In `#gchat-login-slot`: "Chatting as <name>", **Log out**, and a small menu (the dots) with **Forget me** and a sentence of what it does. Both are a `fetch` POST of this page to the hub (`/hub/logout`, `/hub/forget`: same origin, with its cookie, no redirect, one at a time); after either the dock drops its socket and connects again at once, as a reader. A POST that does not work says "That did not work, try again" and changes nothing. The name is text (cleaned and cut like a line's), and nothing of this is kept in the browser.
- **The round trip and a room.** The tab leaves for Discord and comes back: the session storage that holds the seat of the room (`ants.lobby`) belongs to the tab and survives it, so the page opens, takes the seat back in the background as after any reload, and shows Home (or the Room, for `next=room`; a link `?room=CODE` also works). The server holds the seat of a dropped page for its usual window, so a login that takes longer than that (a first visit that reads Discord's page for a minute) finds the seat gone, like any visitor who was away that long: the page then offers the room's usual Rejoin or makes no room, and Home is as it was. `tests/scripts/web_home_login_browser.js` presses it through in a real browser against a real game server.
