# Computer players that are stuck, and what they do about it

Two matches of computer players showed ants that stood for minutes and said "Can't go there." again and again. In the first (a map of the library, four hills, two fire walls and a field of map bombs between the halves) a Thief of one team raided a hill in the other half, took 50 points and stood on the raid tile for the remaining ten minutes with the loot. In the second (a map of the library with four quarters, each shut off from the next by a wall whose gaps are filled with power-ups) Thieves of every team did the same, and the ants that the map had dealt into the other quarters stood still. This page says what the engine does there, which rules the standard bot ([`BOTS.md`](BOTS.md#the-standard-bot)) plays now, how a stuck ant is counted, and what the rules measured.

## What the engine does

- **The order that puts a Thief into the can't-go loop is the engine's own, not a bot's.** At the end of the raid clip a Thief that holds loot is ordered to its own entrance (`raid_clip_end`, the original's `0x101efbd`; the recorded bot gave no order after turn 4321, the raid order, and the loop began at turn 4430). When no walk leads there the walk is refused and the can't-go clip plays (18 ticks for a Thief); at its end an ant that stands on a special tile of a hill (the queue row, the ramp, the entrance, the raid tile; of any hill) is ordered to its own hill's waiting tile (`GoTo(+0x42)`, `0x101f190`: [`BOT_PLAY.md`](BOT_PLAY.md#the-cant-go-loop); `docs/GAME_REVERSE_ENGINEERING.md`, 5.40, "After the clip"; the steps of the actions 5 and 0xd in [`docs/reverse_engineering/movement/03_walk_step_arrival_and_stop.md`](reverse_engineering/movement/03_walk_step_arrival_and_stop.md)), which is refused again. The remake does what those pages say; there is no difference to report (the program itself is not in this checkout, so nothing was read from it again). An ant in the clip is not one that takes an order (`can_take_user_order` accepts the actions 0, 1 and 3, the clip is action 0x0B), so what decides whether an order can get in is the tick between two clips. Until the rules number 14 the engine answered the request of a clip's end before the next turn's commands, so no order ever met the ant: the ant was Walking for the one tick in 19 in which a search bigger than one slice of the path manager was pending, which a bot could not aim at, and when the search was answered at once (a small region: the north half of POPcOrN) it was in the clip at every tick (the recording played on with an order at every tick for 400 ticks: none taken). From 14 the answer to a walk that an event asked for is given after the next turn's commands (`docs/GAME_REVERSE_ENGINEERING.md`, 5.40, "The gap before the path manager's answer"): one tick of each cycle of 18 (the 17 ticks of the clip, then the boundary) finds the ant idle with a walk that nobody has answered, and a move order given at that tick is taken and replaces the held answer (a Stop is skipped). A move to a tile that can be reached ends the loop, as does a walk home that opens. A Thief steals whether or not it can get home (a raid takes up to 50 points from the hill it raids whatever happens to the Thief afterwards).
- **A power-up tile is solid.** No walk crosses it (rule R4 of `can_enter`); the one order that may name it is the pick-up, and the ant that arrives takes it and becomes the Thief, Combat Ant, Bomber, Fire Ant or Swimmer that it makes. A power-up in a gap of a wall, a corridor or a doorway therefore shuts it like a rock until some ant takes it. The analysis of the bots (`MapInfo::walkable`) already read it so: the walkable parts of such a map are separate, and so are the ants in them.
- **The way over a power-up without taking it** exists in the original and in the remake: an order given to another tile inside the cancel window of a pick-up (the 200 ms between the crossing of the tile's edge and the landing) snaps the ant onto the tile and leaves without the pick-up (`tests/test_sim/test_powerup_actions.cpp`, 1.3, 1.5), and the tunnel hop of the corner rows of ISLANDS is a chain of such orders (1.14); an ant that stands on a power-up and is clicked onto its own tile takes it (1.7, 1.18). The bots do not use it for a plug: see "What is left".

## The rules

All of them are plan switches that ship on (the free-thief rule at Hard, the others at every level) and that `bot_arena --tune` turns off for a comparison (`plugs=0`, `refmem=0`, `rescue=0`, `freethief=0`; `unstick=0` is all four off, the bot as it was).

- **A power-up that plugs the way is taken by one ant** (`PlugTask`, `LevelPlan::plugs`). Every 100 ticks (from tick 60) the task labels the walkable parts of the map as it is now, finds the power-ups that touch two of them one of which is the part of the own hill, and when the other part holds an own ant or a pile with food sends one ant to take the power-up: the ant of the far part first (it was stranded, with nothing else to do; walks of up to 1,600 ticks), else the nearest empty-handed ant of the hill's part that no task of a higher rank holds (up to 500 ticks). Only an ant of the level's default type is sent (a typed ant would lose its type), and a power-up that opens nothing for the seat (no own ant, no food behind it), one that another ant stands on, and one that the trip task has an ant on its way to are left alone. The ant keeps the type it takes; the way is open for everybody.
- **A Fire Ant puts out the wall that shuts in an own Thief** (`LevelPlan::rescue_thief`, `note_shut_in_thieves`, `shuts_in_own_thief`). An own Thief that holds loot and has shown the can't-go clip on the raid tile of a hill for 200 ticks (`rescue_after`: a refusal that ends by itself is not walled in) makes the three walls in front of that hole harm to the economy, like the walls in front of the own gate, so the wall task sends its Fire Ant to put one out (the counters of [`BOT_TACTICS.md`](BOT_TACTICS.md#fire-play-contest-batch)), and the plan wants a Fire Ant while such a Thief stands, which the trip task fetches from the Fire power-up of the own side (the wish changes nothing in the matches measured). The Fire Ant is not sent while an ant of another team stands within 5 tiles of the wall (`rescue_guard`: a person looks first; the Fire Ant that walks across the map to a hole with ants beside it is lost, and with it the worker it was made from: see Measured). When the wall is out the engine's own walk home takes the Thief out with its loot.
- **A Thief that shows the can't-go loop is clicked again and again, as a person does it** (`FreeThiefTask`, `LevelPlan::free_thief`: Hard; `Orders::volley`, `Intent::volley`). A person who sees his Thief stand in the clip clicks it onto a tile where it can go until it walks; the one tick of each cycle that takes the click (above) is a window of 50 ms, which he hits by clicking about five times a second. The task does that from what its looks show, the Thief's state and nothing of the engine's queue: a Thief with loot that shows the can't-go clip at two looks over 40 ticks (two cycles: a refusal that a wall about to burn out gives is not a loop) gets a VOLLEY, eight plain moves of that one ant, 3 and 4 ticks apart (24 ticks, a cycle and a third), onto the tile of its walkable part (the map as it is now, nearest first, at most 400 tiles) that lies nearest to the own hill and is not a tile of any hill (the mound, the row in front of it, the raid tile and the waiting tile: the engine sends an ant on one of those home again, so a Thief clicked onto one would loop again). The controller releases each click `later` ticks after the release time of the look (`Intent::later`; never sooner than the reaction time lets any click of a look leave), and each is an order of its own that the bucket pays and holds back (`Fate::Expired` when it cannot be paid in time), as any click: eight tokens a volley, which is why only Hard has it (Medium's bucket holds six). A volley after which the Thief still shows the clip at a look 40 ticks later is a miss and the wait for the next doubles (200 ticks, at most 800); a Thief that walks is free, and the clicks of the volley that come after the one that took are repeats of the same order, which the engine skips. With a sink that applies at once the eight clicks reach eight of the eighteen ticks of the cycle and every volley starts at another phase of it; with the arena's turns of two ticks the clicks are applied on even ticks and the cycle keeps the parity of its moments, so the Thieves of one parity are freed and the others never are (a room of one tick a turn reaches them all). A Thief shut in on the raid tile alone, or in a part of the map that holds only tiles of a hill, has no tile to go to and no volley is given: the Fire Ant above is its way out. When a free Thief with loot stands idle and a walk home exists (the walls are out, the field is as `MapInfo::reaches_hill` says), the task clicks it onto the entrance, as the economy clicks a carrier that waits, no oftener than every 120 ticks. A Thief whose way home is open is never clicked away (AI57.10).
- **A click that the engine refused twice is not given a third time for a while** (`RefusalMemory`, `LevelPlan::refusal_memory`). An order that left and was not carried out (the ant stands on the tile it stood on, idle or in the can't-go clip, 40 ticks later, and no path is on its way) is a refusal of that kind of click by that ant onto that tile. The first refusal blocks nothing (a team-mate on the doorway, a bomb that goes off, a wall that burns out make a click work the next time); the second blocks it for 600 ticks, the third for 1,200, and so on to 4,800. The orders of a look are looked over before they leave and the blocked ants are taken out of them; the tasks are told as for a click that the controller refuses (`Fate::Filtered`), so none proposes it again at every look. Carriers, clicks on or beside the ant's own tile and clicks into a mound (the walk home ends in the hill's queue, which is no refusal) are never judged, and a timed chain is never touched.

## What is left

- **A Thief that is shut in by walls** on the raid tile of a hole whose team lights the three tiles in front of it after the raid stays in the loop until a wall burns out or a wall is put out (the walls of a thief hole closing behind a Thief are what they are for, [`BOT_PLAY.md`](BOT_PLAY.md#the-cant-go-loop)): the Fire Ant of the Thief's team is sent to put one out, see "The rules".
- **The pass over a plug without taking it** (the cancel window above) would keep the type of a Thief that crosses. It is not built: the plug is opened once for everybody by the ant that takes it, which costs one worker its plain type and no more, and the timed chain it needs (a window of two ticks inside one of three) is the most fragile order of the bots.
- **A Thief that raids a hole it cannot get home from** (the first match: a hole behind two fire walls and a field of map bombs, with the Fire power-ups of that map behind the bombs) is clicked away from the raid tile when its part of the map has a tile to go to, and then stands on an ordinary tile with its loot until a walk home opens (the economy's Fire Ant, a wall that burns out) and the task sends it. It does not bank before that. Thieves steal no matter what; on a map whose gaps are plugged the opened plugs are what lets such a raid bank.
- **Ants that the map deals behind a wall of fire and bombs** have no power-up of the right kind on their side to break through with; they stand where they are.

## The counter

`bot_arena` counts the ants that stand nowhere (`StuckTally`, `ArenaResult::stuck`; the counter changes nothing in a match): an own ant is stuck when for 10 seconds in a row it has stood on one tile and was in a state that goes nowhere, which is the can't-go clip (*looping*), an order that does not move it (*blocked*) or food or loot in its hands with no order, more than 8 tiles from its own mound (*carriers*: the engine's walk home that ended at once, which the economy sends home after 900 ticks, [`BOTS.md`](BOTS.md#the-standard-bot); the carriers that wait for the gate stand about the mound, a wait of 700 ticks is legitimate, and are not counted). An ant in a clip of its own work (a raid, a plant, a fire wall, a pick-up, a bite, the hill's entrance) or queueing for its hill is not. The report (format 8) has per seat `stuck` (`ants`, `ticks`, `looping`, `blocked`, `carriers`) and `refused_again` (refused orders that give a first ant the same kind of order onto the same tile as an order that was refused before); a run prints the totals, and `--stuck-detail` prints one line for each of the first stuck spans of a match.

The volley works in the engine's loop (AI57.10: with a sink that applies at once the Thief walks within two volleys in five of six phases of the cycle for the first volley and is not freed in the sixth within 1,000 ticks) and is rare and of little effect in matches. A Thief that raided a hole whose three east tiles are walls stands on the raid tile with the mound and the walls round it: its part of the map is that one tile, and no order leaves it (a move onto its own tile, onto a tile of the mound, onto a tile on the far side of the mound and a Stop were each tried at the boundary: refused or skipped), so no volley is given and the Fire Ant is the way out. Most loops of TREASURE are of that kind. With the arena's turns of two ticks the clicks reach the Thieves of one parity of the cycle and never the others, a sink of one tick a turn reaches all. A freed Thief that walks to its own gate and loops there again is the gate's (the task keeps to Thieves 8 tiles or more from the own entrance, and gives a Thief six volleys and six clicks home in a match): in the first version of the task such a Thief was clicked away and home again 29 times in one match of POPcOrN.

**AI51.9** (four Hard bots on TREASURE, with and without the detour and the Bomber's route, score with / without): seeds 1 to 24 with the unstick batch off 191,630 / 195,490 (98.0 percent), the volley alone 192,525 / 191,730 (100.4 percent), the shipped bots with the whole batch 194,800 / 194,875 (100.0 percent, 403 bombs walked into against 454); seeds 25 to 120 (96 matches) 100.0, 100.4 and 100.3 percent; all 120 seeds 99.6, 100.4 and 100.2 percent. The 2 percent that seeds 1 to 24 show with the batch off is a draw of that sample (24 seeds swing about 2 percent), and the thresholds of the test did not change. The matches were not played under the rules before 14, so what these rules cost is not shown by any of this.

**The Fire Ant's rescue under the rules number 14.** With the whole batch four Hard bots without the thief batch (`thief_batch=0`, the plan of AI51.9, whose Thieves raid holes that are shut) banked 742,915 points in 96 matches of TREASURE, 2.3 percent less than the 760,380 without `rescue` (2.9 percent less than the batch off), and two bots with `rescue` against two without scored 1,931 : 1,983 and 1,946 : 1,984 (288 and 576 matches, rotated, wins 137 : 149 and 295 : 274): about 2 percent, at the edge of what 96 to 576 matches can show. The score was not what fell: the food was (745,320 against 763,250 banked), with 32 ants fewer at the end (1,417 against 1,449) and 37 more kills and losses, while the can't-go reactions fell by a third and the stuck ant-time by half. The Fire Ant that goes to put the walls of another hill's hole out is the keeper of the walls, a worker made into a Fire Ant, and it crosses the map to a hole whose team often has ants beside it: it dies there, and the economy is one ant short. The wish for a Fire Ant is not the cause (the same matches with the wish alone off are the same matches, move for move), nor is a refusal that ends by itself (a loaded Thief that has shown the clip for 200 ticks is what triggers it now: the cost did not move for that, 1,962 : 1,994 and 1,946 : 2,007 two against two). The cause is the trip: the Fire Ant is not sent to a wall that has an ant of another team within 5 tiles (`rescue_guard`), and the cost is gone: 762,295 points in the 96 matches (0.25 percent more than without `rescue`), two against two 2,019 : 2,005 and 1,995 : 1,985 (wins 141 : 147 and 297 : 276). With the shipped bots (the thief batch on, where Thieves do not walk into holes that are shut) `rescue` was neutral before and is now: 1,581,475 points with it against 1,584,235 without (192 matches), two against two 2,084 : 2,078 (288 matches, wins 147 : 140); on SMALL, MEDIUM, ISLANDS, POPcOrN, HELLRAISER and the map with plugged gaps the matches are the same with and without it (the rule never fires there).
