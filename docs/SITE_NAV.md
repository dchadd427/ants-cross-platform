# The bar at the top of every page

Every page of the site except the game itself starts with one bar (the game page, which holds the game, has it too: ["The game page"](#the-game-page)), so that each page has a way to the others: **Matches** (`/matches`), **Match** (`/match`), **Legal** (`/legal`), **Changelog** (`/changelog`), **Map maker** (`/mapmaker`) and **Sprites and sounds** (`/asset_catalog/`). The front page has its own top bar and its own Menu (`web/home/home.js`), which lists the same links. The files are `web/site_nav.css` and `web/site_nav.js` (at the root of `web/`, like `matches.css`: the folder `web/front/` has no room left), each copied once by the `Dockerfile`.

## The markup

Each page carries the same `<header class="sb">`: a **Home** button (with a "<" mark; the bar has no logo, and no page downloads a picture for it), a `<nav aria-label="Pages">` of four links, a flexible gap, the live mark and the **Menu** button. The title of the page and one line about it are one block under the bar (`<div class="pt">`, the page's only `h1`). Match keeps a "< All matches" link above its title. `data-live` on the bar says when the count of live matches is asked for: `now` (Matches and Match: when the page opens), or `menu` (the others: when the Menu is first opened).

- **The live mark** (`a.sblive`, at every width): a small red "N live" link with a dot, shown only while at least one match runs. It links to `/` (the front page lists the running matches, each with Watch; the front page has no address of its own for the list) and is named for a screen reader with the words it shows first ("2 live: see them on the front page"; "1 live: ..."), and carries a `title` for the mouse ("2 matches are being played - see them on the front page"). Its dot stays seen in forced-colors mode (it takes the button's text color). It is not there when the count is zero, when `/live` fails or has not answered yet, or on the front page (which has its own bar and no mark). It reads the one request the bar makes (`/live`, at `data-live`); when the tab comes back to the front (`visibilitychange`) the bar asks again, so a tab left open does not keep an old count (an answer that cannot be read hides the mark); a page that waits for the Menu and has not asked yet does not ask on a return.
- **A phone** (under 720 px): the bar is the Room bar of the front page: Home, the live mark and the Menu. The row of links is not shown.
- **From 720 px up:** the same markup shows the row as stamped buttons: Home, Matches, Map maker and the Discord mark (a new tab, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`). The page that is open is gold and carries `aria-current="page"` (Matches for Matches and Match). The Home button of the phone is not shown. The Menu holds the small links (Sprites and sounds, Changelog, GitHub, Feedback, Legal), and the four again, so that it is one list at every width.
- **A phone held sideways** (under 481 px high): the title and its line share one row.
- **The Menu** (`#sbm`) is built by `site_nav.js` from the bar's own links and the small ones. It opens under the bar, no taller than the window leaves; **Escape** closes it and the focus goes back to its button; a press anywhere else, a new width of the window (turning the phone) or a link closes it too (a change of height alone, which a phone's address bar makes, only fits it to the window again); the button says `aria-expanded`. In the Menu the page that is open (Legal, Changelog, Sprites and sounds, or one of the four) is gold with `aria-current`.
- **The last line of the Menu** is the version and the build of the site, small and quiet, as text: "Version v1.2.3 - build abc1234 (2026-01-02 09:30 PT)". The build stamps the three values into the bar as `data-version`, `data-build` and `data-built` (the Dockerfile, `build_web.sh` and the changelog tool do it, the way they filled the footers before) and `site_nav.js` writes the line with `textContent`. A value that is not there, or still holds its `@@` placeholder (a page of the repository that nobody built), is left out: with no version there is no site line (a build with no version shows nothing), with a version and no build the line is the version alone, and a build with no date has no brackets. The map maker's bar also has `data-maker` (its own version, from `web/MAPMAKER_VERSION`, which the image build checks against the page) and the line ends with "Map maker v1.8.0". Only the front page and the game page have a footer; the pages with the bar have none, so they end with their content, and the credit line ("Ants 1998 Native Engine Remake & Port ...") is on the Legal page.
- **Nothing moves** when the count arrives: the mark sits in the flexible gap, so the other buttons keep their places.

Every button is 44 px tall at least. A forced-colors window gets an outline for the open page and the open Menu, and the bar has no motion. The bar adds no inline style and no inline script (the admin page's policy has neither, and the other pages keep to it): the Menu is placed with `element.style.top`, not a style string.

## Where it is

- Matches and Match (`web/matches.html`, `web/match.html`) replace their old header and lost their footer. Their stylesheet `web/matches.css` lost the old header's rules and the footer's, and `web/front/classic.css` lost `.site-head`.
- Legal (`web/legal.html`) and the changelog (`tools/changelog_to_html.py`) use the bar with a title block; neither has a footer (the changelog's "Generated from CHANGELOG.md ..." sentence is in its title block, the Legal page has the credit line in "Source code and credits").
- Map maker (`web/mapmaker.html`): the bar is above the editor's own row (the map's name and its buttons); the editor has no footer line any more (its version, and the site's, are the last line of the Menu), and its panels keep a margin at the foot that clears the screen's frame. The editor's own phone button, which was called Menu, is called **File**, so that a page has one Menu.
- The Map maker's own screens cover or replace the bar: **My maps** lies over the whole editor from 601 px up, so the bar is taken out of the Tab order and away from a screen reader while it is up (a `:has()` rule on the editor in `web/front/mapmaker.css`), and the screen has its own **Home** button beside **Back to the map** (a phone's My maps is in the page's flow, with the bar and its Home in view, so it has no second one). **Crop to fit** hides the whole editor, bar included, and is a step that is left by **Cancel** (or Escape): it has no link to the front page, and nothing behind it can be reached with the Tab key.
- Sprites and sounds (`asset_catalog/index.html`): the bar is above the catalog's own header (its title and its three tabs); the page had no footer and is not stamped, so its Menu has no version line. The catalog's Play and GitHub buttons are in the bar's Menu now.
- The admin page (`web/admin.html`) has the bar too, with the same markup and the stamps that the Dockerfile and `build_web.sh` write into the others' (the Menu shows the build; the page's Status reads the same stamps). The bar links out of it and nothing links to it. Its title block, the person's chip and the tabs are drawn by its script for a visitor who is let in, so a visitor who is not sees the bar and one card ([`MAP_SUBMISSIONS.md`](MAP_SUBMISSIONS.md#the-admin-page)).

## The game page

`web/shell.html`, which serves a game against computer players, a joined online match, a recording (`?replay=`) and a live view (`?live=`), has the same bar. Its files are `web/game_bar.css` and `web/game_bar.js` (this page loads them after `site_nav.css` and `site_nav.js`); the Dockerfile, `build_web.sh` and the image check carry them.

- **The look.** Before a match, in a match and in a joined room: Home (the front page, with "<" on a phone), the row of four links from 720 px up, the Settings gear, Fullscreen and the Menu button. A recording or a live view: Home, All matches, its title and tools (Copy link, Stats and Download from 701 px up), Fullscreen, Menu. The bar is as high as the header it replaced, with the same room under it (44 px buttons and 18, 16 or 14 px; a recording's buttons keep the sizes that header had), so the picture, the touch buttons and the countdown button keep their places. The page has no footer; its version and build are the last line of the Menu, and a hidden stamp (`#game-version-line`) stays for the image build to find. The Settings gear stands right before Fullscreen.
- **The links.** While a match runs on the page or a room is joined, Home (and the live mark, which goes to the front page too) asks first with the page's question and Yes lets go of the seat; every other link of the bar and of the Menu opens in a new tab (a link that is one anyway is left alone), so no link can lose a match. Otherwise the links act as on any page.
- **A live view that the front page opened in a new tab** (Watch in the Games list, while the page held a room so that its seat stays; [`HOME_GAMES.md`](HOME_GAMES.md)) must not become a second front page: its **Home** (the button, and the Menu's Home) closes the tab and leaves the front page's own tab as it was (never reloaded or navigated, the room kept). The tab is told apart by what the front page says, not by its address or an opener (the link has none: `noopener`): the live view asks on the BroadcastChannel `ants-watch` when it opens, and a front page that listed this match while it held a room answers. Home asks again with a new token, and only an answer that carries it counts, so a front page that has gone since is never taken for one. With an answer Home calls `window.close()`; with none within 0.3 seconds, or when the tab is still there 0.4 seconds after `close()`, it goes on as the plain link of this tab. A live view that no front page answered when it opened (the only tab, one opened by hand, a front page that has lost its room) is a plain link at once, with no wait, and so is a press with Ctrl, Cmd, Shift or Alt, or a middle press. A message cannot bring another tab to the front (a page cannot raise another tab, and `focus()` is ignored without a gesture there), so it is the closing of the tab that returns the person: a browser goes back to the tab that a closed tab was opened from.
- **What this relies on in a browser.** A tab that a link opened (`target="_blank"`) may be closed by its own script, however many pages it has shown (the link's `noopener` does not change that); `tests/scripts/web_host_return_browser.js` (opt-in, a real Chromium and a real game server) runs the whole way: Watch in a room, Home in the watch tab, and the room's tab untouched. Not run, and left to each browser's own rules: Safari and the iPhone, Chrome on Android, an installed web app ([`INSTALL_AS_APP.md`](INSTALL_AS_APP.md)), and which tab a browser shows after the close. Where a browser refuses `close()` Home goes on as a link, and nothing is lost: the first tab keeps its seat, and a plain visit makes no room. A front page tab that the browser has frozen or discarded does not answer, so Home is then a link.
- **The keys.** The game takes every key (Enter is START on its setup screen). While the Menu is open or the focus is in the bar, the keys are the bar's: they stop at the document before the game sees them, a key whose press the bar kept stays the bar's until its lift (a held key goes on repeating after the Menu has closed), Esc closes the Menu and the game never hears it, Enter and Space press the focused control, and a bare Shift, Ctrl, Alt or Meta goes on to the game. A press with the pointer gives the focus back to the game, and so does closing the Menu or the question any way (its button, Esc, a press outside, Cancel; a press outside has to start and end outside the card: a drag from inside the card or a scroll does not close it): the keys are the bar's only while it is open or the focus was moved into the bar with Tab. The bar and the Menu are controls of the page: a pointer on their blank parts never scrolls the map from the picture's edge.
- **The Menu's own lines** are made each time it opens: a note while a match runs ("Home asks first; the other links open in a new tab"), and for a recording on a phone **Copy link**, **Download** (not for a live view; a file of your own has neither Copy link nor Download) and, for a file of your own, **Open another file**, each pressing the bar's own button; **Reload page** last among the small links: it reloads at once, except while a game is being played (a match on this device or online; not before a match, not in a recording or a live view), when a small question asks first ("Reload the page? A game against the computer on this device will end; an online match comes back by itself.", Cancel focused).

## The front page's Menu

`web/home/home.js` lists the same links in the same order (Home, Matches, Map maker, How it works, Discord server, then Sprites and sounds, Changelog, GitHub, Feedback, Legal). Home is the first item and is marked as the page you are on (in the Room it goes back to Home without loading the page again). The Menu has no live count: the games list of the front page shows the running matches. `tests/scripts/test_web_site_nav.py` keeps the two lists the same.

## Tests

- `tests/scripts/test_web_site_nav.py` reads every page: one bar, the same four links, the page that is open marked, the markup of the phone's pieces, no inline style or script, the files in the `Dockerfile`, the byte budgets of the two files (`NAV_*` in `tools/size_limits.py`), plain ES5, the lists of Home and of the bar the same, and the old header's rules gone. Its mutants are in `tests/data/site_nav_mutants.json`.
- `tests/scripts/web_site_nav_browser.js` (opt-in, needs Playwright and a Chromium) runs the pages in a real browser at 320, 390, 450, 721, 768 and 1280 px wide and 667 x 375, with none and two live: no sideways scroll, nothing past the window, no overlap in the bar, 44 px buttons, the phone's and the wide shape, the page that is open marked, the live count, nothing moving when it arrives, and the Menu (opens, Escape and focus, a press outside). `--shots DIR` takes a picture of every page at every size.
- `tests/scripts/test_web_site_nav.py` also runs the Menu in node (`tests/scripts/web_site_nav_menu_check.js`, the real `site_nav.js` on the real markup with a small fake of the DOM): the last line of a stamped page, a page nobody stamped (no line, never a placeholder), a stamp with the version only, every link the old footers had, and no footer on the pages with the bar.
- The game page's own part is `tests/scripts/test_web_game_bar.py` (the files, the byte budget `GAME_BAR_*` in `tools/size_limits.py`, and a real Chromium run of `tests/scripts/web_game_bar_check.js` on the real `site_nav.js` and the real markup: the links, the way back of a live view that the front page opened, the keys, the focus, the Menu's lines, the room under the bar); its mutants are `tests/data/game_bar_mutants.json`.
- The pages' own tests moved with the change: `test_web_matches.py`, `test_web_legal.py`, `test_web_catalog.py`, `test_changelog_to_html.py`, `test_web_replay_open.py` and `web_home_screen_check.js`.
