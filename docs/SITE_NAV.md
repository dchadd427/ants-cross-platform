# The bar at the top of every page

Every page of the site except the game itself and the admin page starts with one bar, so that each page has a way to the others: **Matches** (`/matches`), **Match** (`/match`), **Live now** (`/live-now`), **Legal** (`/legal`), **Changelog** (`/changelog`), **Map maker** (`/mapmaker`) and **Sprites and sounds** (`/asset_catalog/`). The front page has its own top bar and its own Menu (`web/home/home.js`), which lists the same links. The files are `web/site_nav.css` and `web/site_nav.js` (at the root of `web/`, like `matches.css`: the folder `web/front/` has no room left), each copied once by the `Dockerfile`.

## The markup

Each page carries the same `<header class="sb">`: a **Home** button (with a "<" mark), the logo (a link to `/`), a `<nav aria-label="Pages">` of five links, a flexible gap, a live button and the **Menu** button. The title of the page and one line about it are one block under the bar (`<div class="pt">`, the page's only `h1`). Match keeps a "< All matches" link above its title. `data-live` on the bar says when the count of live matches is asked for: `now` (Matches and Match: when the page opens), `menu` (the others: when the Menu is first opened) or nothing (Live now, which lists them itself).

- **A phone** (under 720 px): the bar is the Room bar of the front page: Home, the logo, a red "N live" button (only while at least one match is live, never on Live now) and the Menu. The row of five links is not shown.
- **From 720 px up:** the same markup shows the row as stamped buttons: Home, Matches, "Live now" (with a dot, and the number of live matches in a round badge when there are some), Map maker and the Discord mark (a new tab, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`). The page that is open is gold and carries `aria-current="page"` (Matches for Matches and Match). The Home button and the live button of the phone are not shown. The Menu holds the small links (Sprites and sounds, Changelog, GitHub, Feedback, Legal), and the five again, so that it is one list at every width.
- **A phone held sideways** (under 481 px high): the title and its line share one row.
- **The Menu** (`#sbm`) is built by `site_nav.js` from the bar's own links and the small ones. It opens under the bar, no taller than the window leaves; **Escape** closes it and the focus goes back to its button; a press anywhere else, a resize or a link closes it too; the button says `aria-expanded`. In the Menu the page that is open (Legal, Changelog, Sprites and sounds, or one of the five) is gold with `aria-current`.
- **The last line of the Menu** is the version and the build of the site, small and quiet, as text: "Version v1.2.3 - build abc1234 (2026-01-02 09:30 PT)". The build stamps the three values into the bar as `data-version`, `data-build` and `data-built` (the Dockerfile, `build_web.sh` and the changelog tool do it, the way they filled the footers before) and `site_nav.js` writes the line with `textContent`. A value that is not there, or still holds its `@@` placeholder (a page of the repository that nobody built), is left out: with no version there is no site line (a build with no version shows nothing), with a version and no build the line is the version alone, and a build with no date has no brackets. The map maker's bar also has `data-maker` (its own version, from `web/MAPMAKER_VERSION`, which the image build checks against the page) and the line ends with "Map maker v1.8.0". Only the front page and the game page have a footer; the pages with the bar have none, so they end with their content, and the credit line ("Ants 1998 Native Engine Remake & Port ...") is on the Legal page.
- **Nothing moves** when the count arrives: the dot and the round badge of the row's Live now button have their room from the start (the dot is dim and the badge empty until the count is above none), and the phone's live button sits in the gap.

Every button is 44 px tall at least. A forced-colors window gets an outline for the open page and the open Menu, and the bar has no motion. The bar adds no inline style and no inline script (the admin page's policy has neither, and the other pages keep to it): the Menu is placed with `element.style.top`, not a style string.

## Where it is

- Matches, Match and Live now (`web/matches.html`, `web/match.html`, `web/live.html`) replace their old header and lost their footer. Their stylesheet `web/matches.css` lost the old header's rules and the footer's, and `web/front/classic.css` lost `.site-head`.
- Legal (`web/legal.html`) and the changelog (`tools/changelog_to_html.py`) use the bar with a title block; neither has a footer (the changelog's "Generated from CHANGELOG.md ..." sentence is in its title block, the Legal page has the credit line in "Source code and credits").
- Map maker (`web/mapmaker.html`): the bar is above the editor's own row (the map's name and its buttons); the editor has no footer line any more (its version, and the site's, are the last line of the Menu), and its panels keep a margin at the foot that clears the screen's frame. The editor's own phone button, which was called Menu, is called **File**, so that a page has one Menu.
- Sprites and sounds (`asset_catalog/index.html`): the bar is above the catalog's own header (its title and its three tabs); the page had no footer and is not stamped, so its Menu has no version line. The catalog's Play and GitHub buttons are in the bar's Menu now.
- The admin page has no bar on purpose: only its logo, and nothing on the site links to it.

## The front page's Menu

`web/home/home.js` lists the same links in the same order (Home, Matches, Live now, Map maker, How it works, Discord server, then Sprites and sounds, Changelog, GitHub, Feedback, Legal). Home is the first item and is marked as the page you are on (in the Room it goes back to Home without loading the page again). The number of live matches comes from the footer's line (`stats-live`), which `/stats` fills in: there is no new request. `tests/scripts/test_web_site_nav.py` keeps the two lists the same.

## Tests

- `tests/scripts/test_web_site_nav.py` reads every page: one bar, the same five links, the page that is open marked, the markup of the phone's pieces, no inline style or script, the files in the `Dockerfile`, the byte budgets of the two files (`NAV_*` in `tools/size_limits.py`), plain ES5, the lists of Home and of the bar the same, and the old header's rules gone. Its mutants are in `tests/data/site_nav_mutants.json`.
- `tests/scripts/web_site_nav_browser.js` (opt-in, needs Playwright and a Chromium) runs the pages in a real browser at 320, 390, 450, 721, 768 and 1280 px wide and 667 x 375, with none and two live: no sideways scroll, nothing past the window, no overlap in the bar, 44 px buttons, the phone's and the wide shape, the page that is open marked, the live count, nothing moving when it arrives, and the Menu (opens, Escape and focus, a press outside). `--shots DIR` takes a picture of every page at every size.
- `tests/scripts/test_web_site_nav.py` also runs the Menu in node (`tests/scripts/web_site_nav_menu_check.js`, the real `site_nav.js` on the real markup with a small fake of the DOM): the last line of a stamped page, a page nobody stamped (no line, never a placeholder), a stamp with the version only, every link the old footers had, and no footer on the pages with the bar.
- The pages' own tests moved with the change: `test_web_matches.py`, `test_web_legal.py`, `test_web_catalog.py`, `test_changelog_to_html.py`, `test_web_replay_open.py` and `web_home_screen_check.js`.
