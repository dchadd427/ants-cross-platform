# Walled maps: the gate goals, the field breach, and POPcOrN

A Medium or Hard computer player plays the **gate goals** on a map whose food its hill cannot walk to at the start (HELLRAISER and its family, POPcOrN): it gets the ant that the way past needs (a Fire Ant for a fire wall, a Bomber for bombs or a rock wall) and does it. The rules of the bombs, the planner and its numbers are in [bomb_gates.md](reverse_engineering/bomb_gates.md#gate-goals-ants_ai-medium-and-hard-walled-maps); the decision and the known limits in [BOTS.md](BOTS.md#walled-maps-the-gate-goals). A pile that lies beyond a field of bombs, a fire wall and water is the **field breach**'s instead (below). This page is about the map's own bombs, the field breach, what was measured, and POPcOrN in a match: a brief for whoever works on it next.

## The map's own bombs are the gate

On a gate map the counters (`BombTask`) leave the bombs of no team (the map's own: no team, 255) alone while the gate goals are live (not in the stall detector's fallback, not exhausted); the gate goals defuse those that are on their way (`LevelPlan::gate_spare_map_bombs`, `bot_arena --tune gatebombs=0` is the bot before). A team's bomb is countered as on any map, and nothing changes on a map that is not a gate map (the gate play is Medium's and Hard's only: Easy is untouched).

Why: on POPcOrN the counters sent workers to set off the map bombs next to the hill (a worker that steps on a bomb is thrown four tiles, into the bomb field: lost) and Bombers to defuse bombs of the field, over and over: most of the bot's orders there were refused, and the ants that the breach needed were walked away or over bombs (the Bomber that had just defused the corridor to the lake was sent to a map bomb in the field, walked over another one and was thrown across the field to its death). The rule reads only what every walled map has (whether the bot plays the gate goals there, and the owner of a bomb); there is no map name, size or coordinate in it.

## The field breach

Some maps put their food beyond a **field**: map bombs, a fire wall and water, so that no walk with only one kind of wall open reaches it (the gate analysis calls such a pile Sealed or Mixed). POPcOrN is one: the pile stands on an island in a lake in the middle of a bomb field, between two fire walls. A Medium or Hard bot opens such a pile with **one ant** that does all the work, in an order derived from the map (`FieldTask`, `field_plan.hpp`): it becomes a Bomber, hops into the field with one bomb where the field has bomb-free ground in reach, digs a corridor, hops onto the pile's island from the dug ground, digs there, becomes a Swimmer and bridges the water that the Fire Ant will cross, becomes a Fire Ant and puts the route's fire cell out, and a Swimmer then carries. `bot_arena --tune field=0` is the bot before (the gate goals' shore breach on those piles), and a bot with the switch off plays every match exactly as the bot of v0.12.59 (state hash identical).

**When** (`StandardBot::decide_gate_map`, once, at the first look of a gate map): `plan_field` finds a pile whose route from the hill crosses fire cells or bombs; every kind of work has a source (a way for a Fire Ant to a side of the route's fire, and for a Swimmer to the land beside the water of that way); and an ant of the bot can get the kind of the first work (`field_startable`; on DeJaVoO the Fire power-ups lie behind the fire wall, so no ant gets a Fire Ant, and the gate goals' raid of the pile banks there as before). The piles that are Sealed or Mixed at that look are the breach's for the whole match (`Tactics::field_piles`): the gate goals leave them alone, also after the work has changed their gates, and play every other pile as before.

**The plan** (`plan_field`, a pure function of the grid and the look):

- **The route**: the carriers' way from the hill to a side of the pile, walking and swimming; its bombs are defused and its fire cells put out first. The cheapest pile of the breach; the route and the sources chosen are kept while they hold, so that the work goes on toward the same targets.
- **The carriers' bombs.** The engine gives a carrier the cheapest walk, on which another team's bomb is no obstacle (an ant that steps on one is thrown), and of walks of the same cost the one its search meets first. So every bomb on a walk that costs at most half a step more than the cheapest one between the hill and a side of the pile is work, and so is every bomb round the pile (a power-up that an ant leaves there turns the walk aside). A bomb of the seat's own color never throws its carriers (it is a wall to them) and is work only where the route needs it.
- **The fire way**: the way of a Fire Ant from its source (a Fire power-up, or a Fire Ant of the bot) to a side of the route's fire cell; it walks through fire walls, and a bomb on it is defused first, its water bridged first. A source whose way has work in a region of the field that none of the route's work is in costs the one ant a hop more. An ant that takes a power-up leaves its own on one of the free land tiles beside it (the engine draws which), and that power-up shuts a way over the tile: from a Fire power-up with n such tiles a way out over one of them counts `kDropShut / n` more, so it leaves over water (bridged) where it can.
- **The swim way**: the way of a Swimmer to the land beside the fire way's water.
- **The work**, in this order: the bombs (a Bomber), the bridges (a Swimmer), the fire (a Fire Ant), and for a route that swims a Swimmer to carry. A Swimmer cannot defuse; a Fire Ant can neither defuse nor swim.

**The steps** (`next_field_step`). The engine's walks go over bombs, and it works an ability from the first free side of the target nearest the ant (N, S, W, E; a bomb counts as free), so the dig asks the engine's own search (`EngineWalks`: the port of the path finder that `BombDetour` uses, on the map as it is): the ant walks to the farthest tile of its way that the engine's walk reaches without crossing a bomb of another team, several tiles at a time, and steps one tile where that walk would cross one; a bomb further on is defused with ONE order (the engine walks to the side of it that it takes first) when the walk to that side crosses no bomb either, and an ant that has no hold on the engine's walk (it finds none: a door that an ant blocks) keeps the old rule, ground with no bomb on or next to the way. `--tune fwalk=0` is the dig as it was. Where no dig reaches the work, a Bomber hops with a bomb of its own: the fewest bombs first (a chain over map bombs stops at a dud one time in five and strands the ant), landing where a dig reaches the work; it leaves the hill's side with at least 8 hit points (it heals first). Where no single hop lands there (a fire wall and then a lake lie between the hill's side and a Swimmer power-up on an island), it hops first to ground from which a second hop does: the second launch is costed like one more hop, and it is looked for only when no single hop exists, so a Bomber that had a hop before takes the same one. No bomb is planted on a tile that an ant stands on, the seat's own or another team's: the engine plants none there, and a special order on such a tile is not a click a person can make (the controller refuses it, an ant in its death clip counts too). An ant that took a power-up never takes another one whose tile has a single free land tile beside it and nothing else its new kind leaves over: the power-up it leaves would shut it in (an ant of the level's kind leaves nothing and may).

**The task** (`FieldTask`): one ant, the one that gets the work's kind soonest (`field_ant_cost`; the hill's side first), held at the rank of the gate goals; one order at a time, to an ant that stands. An ant whose order the world does not carry out (the same order four times with nothing of the ant changed, or 45 seconds with no change) is given up and rests two minutes; a step that is Stuck first gets one fresh plan (the route, the ways and the sources kept from before are dropped; at most three to one ant; `--tune freplan=0` gives the ant up at once) and the ant is given up only if the fresh plan is Stuck as well; after four ants given up the task waits until the bot's ants or the power-ups change. The bombs of the seat's own color that lay on the map at the task's first look are work like any other (many levels give the map's bombs to team 0); a bomb planted since, also on a tile where one of them was, is the ant's own (a hop's) and a wall.

**An ant on its power-up** (`--tune fpick=0` is the breach as it was). An ant that stands on the power-up the work needs (a flight puts an ant on one without taking it, and an order that is cancelled in the pick-up window leaves it there) takes it with a click on its own tile, as a person's click does: the engine takes it within about 200 ms (`tests/test_sim/test_powerup_actions.cpp` 1.7; a Stop inside the window followed by the click on the tile while the ant moves gives the same, 1.18, and a Stop alone never takes it, 1.13). Such an ant counts as one that can start the work (`field_ant_cost`; before, a power-up that any ant stood on, the ant's own too, was nobody's), and an ant that can do nothing else and stands on a Swimmer power-up takes that one: a Swimmer swims out of a ring of bombs and water, where before the ant was given up again and again for good. Where nothing round the ant is free the old way, off the tile and back on, was no way at all.

**The power-up an ant takes** (`--tune fserve=0` is the breach as it was). An ant takes a power-up of the kind the work needs only if the kind it becomes is not shut off from the work: `plan_field` lists the power-ups that serve it (`FieldPlan::serving`), those from whose tile a search with the moves of the kind reaches a tile of the route or of the ways. A Swimmer swims and a Fire Ant walks through fire (and has the water on its way bridged); the bombs on the way of either count as work that the Bomber does, so only fire, rock and the map's edge shut a power-up off. The route starts on the hill's side, so the test keeps out pockets (a Swimmer power-up on a strip between a fire wall and the edge of the map, below) and not the power-ups of another part of the field. An ant that can reach only a power-up that does not serve is not chosen to start the work (`field_ant_cost`), and a Bomber's hop aims at the sides of the serving ones only.

**A seat that has no ant to start the work** (`--tune fsetoff=0` is the breach as it was). When no ant of the bot reaches a power-up of the kind the work needs without crossing a bomb for 300 ticks, a plain ant (one that took no power-up, the level's own kind: a Worker, on POPcOrN a Combat Ant; a typed ant would lose its kind, and a Bomber defuses; not one with food) crosses ONE bomb of another team by setting it off: it steps onto it from a side of it, the engine's throw (`GateInfo::simulate`, the rule of the engine's `pick_landing`) must land it on ground, not in water or fire, and it must keep 3 hit points after the 2 that each bomb of the chain takes; at most two set-offs in 600 ticks, and a way across a second bomb is no way. An ant on a Swimmer power-up that nothing else frees counts as a start in the same state. The rule has little to do on POPcOrN: in a field of about 250 bombs a power-up behind one bomb is rare (it did not fire in 40 matches of four Hard bots and fired once in 40 of Medium), and the stalled seats there are mostly ants shut in on an island or with every power-up of the kind taken. On the hand-made worlds it is what takes the power-up behind one bomb (AI29.17).

**The carriers** are the ferry's Swimmers (`FerryTask`). On these maps the ferry also sends again a Swimmer whose loop ended away from the pile: its walk back found no way, another ant stood in the one-tile corridor. Elsewhere the ferry is unchanged.

### The route, and the engine facts it rests on

**The route** (in neutral words; POPcOrN is the map it was found on): the pile lies on an island in a lake in the middle of a field of map bombs, between two fire walls, and the bot's own half has Bomber power-ups but none for the Fire Ant. One ant does the work: (1) a Bomber plants one bomb and hops over the first fire wall onto a bomb-free pocket of the field (a hop of one bomb, with at least 8 hit points: it heals first); (2) it digs: it defuses the bombs of the carriers' walk and of the way of the Fire Ant, one tile at a time; (3) from dug ground a second hop lands on the pile's island, and it digs there; (4) as a Swimmer it bridges the water that the Fire Ant will cross; (5) a Fire Ant puts the route's fire cell out, last, from the field side; (6) the ferry's Swimmers carry the food home. The planner (`plan_field`) derives all of it from the grid, so no map is named in it.

**Engine facts the planner and the steps respect:**

- Paths and ability tiles can lie on map bombs, and the engine works an ability from the first free side of the target nearest the ant, so in the field the ant moves tile by tile and works a target from the side it stands on; it walks several tiles only over ground with no bomb on or next to the way.
- A power-up an ant leaves behind when it takes another lands on a random free land tile next to the one taken. It can shut the Fire Ant's only way out. So the planner prefers a way out over a bridge (water takes no power-up), never takes a power-up at the end of a corridor, and gives an ant up after two power-ups taken in turn (each leaves the other where the ant must pass).
- Carriers take any cheapest walk, ties included, so every bomb on any cheapest walk (within half a step) is dug too, and so is every bomb round the pile.
- A hop over map bombs stops at a dud about one time in five and strands the ant; the fewest bombs go first.

## POPcOrN in a match

**The map.** The hills of teams 0 and 2 are in the north half, those of teams 1 and 3 in the south half. A fire wall runs across each half's edge; between the walls lies a field of about 250 map bombs with a lake in the middle, and the only food, 50 units of 50 points, lies on an island in the lake. Each half has Bomber, Thief and Combat power-ups but no Fire power-up; the field has 5 Swimmer, 3 Fire and 2 Bomber power-ups. The start ants are Combat Ants.

**With the field breach** a bot alone banks in every game, and in a match the bots score (the table above): about 1,140 points a seat one against one, 570 with four bots. The seats are not equal. **The start ants are dealt across both halves**: teams 0 and 1 have one start ant in their own half and two in the other, teams 2 and 3 two at home and one away, and in a match every half holds Combat Ants of two to four teams, which fight at once (the home ants attack the strangers first). A breach needs an ant of the own half for its first Bomber, so the seats with one home ant start later or not at all: with four bots seat 2 scores in 7 of 8 games, seat 0 in 6, seat 3 in 3 and seat 1 in 2. The breach ant is held at the rank of the gate goals, above the fights, but a fight can still kill it.

**The breach before** (`--tune field=0`: the gate goals' shore breach) hopped a Bomber onto the field's Fire power-up over two bombs, bounced home over map bombs, put out the fire cell at the shore end of the walk to the lake, defused that walk's bombs with a home Bomber again, and sent a Swimmer reached by another hop: alone it got through in about a third of the games, and in a match never (the home ant died in the fights before it had its Bomber, chains stopped at duds and stranded ants, a flight onto a power-up took nothing, and the wall keeper and the power-up trips took the time).

**Tried and left out** (Hard, against the bot without it):

- **The carriers' walks predicted with the engine's own search** (`sim::PathSearch`, from every tile a carrier leaves the hill by to every cell of the pile) instead of every walk within half a step of the cheapest: the walk the engine gives depends on the power-ups an ant leaves and the bridges of the moment, so the bombs to defuse changed while the breach went on; alone on POPcOrN 2,086 points against 2,214.
- **A landing that a dig from reaches all the island's work** (a hop is valued one hop dearer when it leaves work out): no change on POPcOrN (2,230 against 2,214), +12 seat-games of 800 on the design half, -2 on the held-out half; left out.
- **A breach started only by an ant on the hill's side**, or only by one that, as the first work's kind, has a step toward the work: each kept pop xx's one bite of 30 points, and each lost the breach on maps whose start ants stand away from their hills (ThE ReVeNgE 2,422 to 0, CrUnChY BiTe 1,262 to 0, !-Midz-! 1,575 to 272, TeAMs OWn 580 to 44).

- **The next goal at once** (an explicit claim of the gate goal on its ant: when a goal is done, plan the next one in the same look and take its ant before the other tasks look, instead of 20 ticks later). Alone on POPcOrN it lifted a Hard bot from 415 to 464 points, but with the rule above it added nothing (606 against 619), and it cost the vault raids of the HELLRAISER family in matches: duels 43.6 +- 9.2 percent of the share on HELLRAISER and 26.0 +- 16.7 on HELLRAISER III (32 seeds x 2 seatings), and 30.3 +- 24.7 on HELLRAISER with four seats. A likely cause, not yet checked: the next raid takes the Bomber at once with the hit points for the way in but not for the way back.
- **No job for a target that no walk reaches** (the counters, the mines, the wall keeper; the old work-in-progress `reach`): it ended the refused orders but cost Ghetto 19 points of share in duels (31.0 +- 14.9 percent, 8 seeds x 2 seatings), ANTS_PARTY and FKD Deathmatch about 5 each, and was no better on POPcOrN than the rule above.
- **A stranded Bomber that defuses its way to the target**: a first version changed nothing (the cases it was meant for ended before it, in a walk home to heal that no walk could make); not finished.
- **Holding the walls and the own side's power-up trips back for the whole breach** instead of the first 2,400 ticks: one seat-match of 32 more (noise).

## Why seats bank nothing

On POPcOrN a seat banks nothing in about a quarter of its matches, and the food is one pile of 2,500 points that nearly every match eats whole, so a seat whose breach is late banks nothing at all. What a bot did in such a match was followed by tick: `bot_arena` for the numbers, and a private tracer (not in the repository) that reads the engine and the bot's ledger of ants every 200 ticks, with the breach task's own state (its ant, its work, the ants it gave up).

**The cause that was found is the stall detector, not the plan of the breach** ([BOTS.md](BOTS.md#the-stall-detector)). Ants die in the field in the first seconds of every match of POPcOrN (the start ants of different teams stand together in the four corners, and a blow throws an ant four tiles, over the fire wall into the field), and a dead ant's lunchbox lies there that no walk reaches. The detector counted every lunchbox as food that the hill can reach, so every seat that had not banked by tick 3,600 was sent to the plain economy: the breach's ant was given back and nothing took it again for 2,400 ticks (doubled at every later stall, at most 9,600), the raids stopped, and the food-gone batch ([BOT_GONE.md](BOT_GONE.md)) was off for the whole fallback. A breach takes about 3,000 ticks when it goes well, so the stall came in the middle of it: 1,299 stalls in 2,400 seat-games, one or two for every seat. `LevelPlan::stall_walkable` makes the detector count only food that the hill's ants walk to (`--tune stallwalk=0` is the rule as it was); 157 stalls are left, most of them twelve equal orders within 1,200 ticks.

**A given-up ant waited out its rest whatever the map did.** An ant whose step is Stuck (no dig, hop or walk reaches the work) rests 2,400 ticks, and the task rests after four such ants until the bot's ants or power-ups change. A bomb defused by the other team, a fire put out or a bridge made opens ground that the ant could not reach before, and none of them is an ant or a power-up of the bot. `LevelPlan::field_wake` (`--tune fwake=0`: as before) ends the rest of an ant, and of the task, when the number of the map's bombs, fire walls or bridges changes.

**Measured** (POPcOrN, Hard and Medium, seeds 1 to 100; mixed levels Medium, Hard, Hard, Medium by seat, four Hard, four Medium; free for all and 2 against 2 with the seats 0 + 2 against 1 + 3; 2,400 seat-games each; every match replay-checked; "before" is the same build with both switches off, which plays every one of the 240 matches that were compared with the state hash of the bot before):

| | seats banking nothing | score of 0 or less | points a seat | idle, all ant time | idle after the last food | power-ups taken | points raided | stalls |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| before | 614 (25.6%) | 718 | 576 | 45.9% | 77.9% | 18,769 | 102,250 | 1,299 |
| after | **500 (20.8%)** | **617** | **589** | **43.7%** | 77.7% | **20,682** | **119,850** | **157** |

The stall detector's rule does most of it (alone, on the same matches: 512 seats banking nothing, 19,238 power-ups taken, 45.1 percent idle). Ending the rests adds power-ups taken in all six configurations (+7.5 percent in all) and a lower idle share in five of them (the sixth: 47.2 to 47.3), but its effect on the seats that bank nothing is inside the noise of the draw (four configurations better by 2 to 11 seats, two worse by 2 and 9). Ants lost 5,319 to 5,555 and forced hatches 1,111 to 1,245: the food-gone batch now begins for a seat that has banked nothing instead of waiting out a fallback, and it fights.

## HELLRAISER with four bots

A reported match of four Medium bots in two teams (seats 0 + 3 against 1 + 2, 15 minutes) ended 0 to 0: one kill a side, a Bomber's bombs defused by its own ally, about 900 can't-go reactions a seat, nobody hatched. In the arena (`bot_arena --map Community-Maps/HELLRAISER.lvl --seeds 1..192 --seat 0=medium ... --teams 0+3`) 78 of 192 matches of that kind gave no point at all, and 52 percent of the seats banked nothing.

**The map.** Four quadrants shut off by a cross of rock, a vault of five by five tiles in the middle (the one pile, 2,500 points; four Bomber, four Combat and four Fire power-ups), a Bomber power-up in each outer corner and a Thief power-up beside each arm. The three start ants of a team are Combat Ants and stand in ANOTHER team's quadrant, beside that team's hill, which has no ant of its own. So a seat's way is: take the corner Bomber power-up, hop into the vault, take the food, hop out into the quadrant of its own hill, bank, heal (an ant that enters its hill has all its hit points), and again. Each hop costs 2 hit points and is a dud one time in five. The hops of the four seats start from the same five tiles.

**What was found** (each by the bomb events, the ants' states and the orders of whole matches):

- **Bombs defused by the ally.** Two allies (Red and Blue) chose the same plant tile, (15,14), from different sides. A special order onto a tile that holds a bomb is a defuse, so the plant order of one seat took the other's bomb away, the other planted again, and so on for the rest of the match (13 and 17 defuses in two of eight matches; both seats stood in the vault with food). `GateGoalTask` knew only its own bombs on the plant tile.
- **Bombers that stand in the middle.** They wait on each other (a hop needs its plant tile and its standing tile free of ants, and no bomb of another team on either), or their hit points are gone: of 64 seats at the end of a match 36 had a Bomber with 2 hit points and food in the vault or in another team's quadrant (a hop needs 3), and 25 had lost it. Where the hit points went: duds (a fifth of the throws), a walk to the standing tile that crossed another team's bomb, a hop from the wrong side of the plant tile, and the Combat Ants of the other team in the home quadrant, which punch a landing Bomber back four tiles, over the wall, for 2 hit points. Bombers keep their blow (one hit point, a throw of one tile; a Combat Ant's is two and four): the bots never use it, a fight takes the level's plain ant and the Combat Ant.
- **No hatching.** An egg costs 200 points and needs 200; a seat reached 200 in few matches, and the hatch is off at every level of every map ([BOTS.md](BOTS.md#aggression)).
- **Few orders and 900 can't-go reactions.** Two of a seat's three start ants have nothing to do (nothing of the other teams is in their quadrant), and a third is the Thief that raids the hill beside it: a raid that takes up to 50 points from that hill, and the Thief cannot walk home through the rock, so the engine's can't-go loop (18 ticks a reaction, [BOTS.md](BOTS.md#the-cant-go-loop)) runs for the rest of the match: 16,000 ticks are 890 reactions. No task loops. Not changed: the raid denies the other team (4,700 points over 192 matches).

**What changed** ([bomb_gates.md](reverse_engineering/bomb_gates.md#gate-goals-ants_ai-medium-and-hard-walled-maps), "Bombs and ants of other teams"): the plant order never goes onto a bomb of another team (another hop is planned); among hops of the same cost the planner prefers one that no ant of another team stands in the way of, that keeps four tiles from the Combat Ants of other teams, whose plant tile is on the side of the Bomber's own hill rather than its ally's, and that is planted from the tile it steps from; the next step of a hop's chain (plant, walk to the tile, throw) follows after 40 ticks, not 120 (the same order again still waits 120: the stall detector counts equal orders; 40 for every order cost Ghetto 8 percent); the power-up trips take a power-up of the ally's side on a gate map when no ant of the ally can walk to it.

**Teams 0 + 1 against 2 + 3** (the start ants of a team stand in its ally's quadrant, and an ally's Combat Ants are in every home quadrant: no punch at all). Before, no seat took a point in any of 48 matches: the only power-up the start ants reach lies on the ally's side, and the power-up trips never take an ally's (BOTS.md, "Stealing is Hard's"). On a gate map such a power-up is taken when no ant of the ally can walk to it (`ally_reaches`): 453 points a seat, 18 of 192 seats banking nothing (AI27.18).

## Tests and mutants

`test_ai_field.cpp` (AI29.1 to AI29.22) draws hand-made worlds for every seat, straight and transposed: the plan and the route, the hop and the dig, the bridge and the fire, the power-up that shuts an ant in, the map's own bombs of the seat's color, who plays it, the breach in the engine, the give-up rules, the ferry's resend, the piles kept, the counters' sparing while the breach is live, the click on the own tile (AI29.15), the dig by the engine's own walks, run in the engine with the bomb's hit points as the proof that no order crosses a bomb (AI29.16), the set-off of a stalled seat (AI29.17), the power-ups that serve the work (a strip in a ring of fire, the island's, one behind bombs: AI29.18), the second hop in a field with no bombs to chain over (AI29.19), the planted tile with an ant of the seat or of another team on it (AI29.20), and the fresh plan before a Stuck ant is given up, in the engine with the switch on and off (AI29.21), and the rest of a given-up ant and of the task that ends when the map's bombs, fire walls or bridges change (AI29.22; AI56.12 in `test_ai_gone.cpp` is the stall detector's no-point rule with a lunchbox behind rock).

`tests/data/bot_field_mutants.json` (69 mutants, `tools/mutate.py --keep-going --jobs 1`): 65 caught. Three survive. Two are backstops that the hand-made worlds do not reach and that changed nothing in the 50-map survey either (8 seeds x every seat, a Hard bot alone: 585 seat-games that bank and 2,207 points a game with them, 584 to 587 and 2,202 to 2,211 with each one off): the cost of a swim in the choice of a source (`swim_way_cost_ignored`) and the 45-second no-progress limit while an ant is on its way (`no_progress_kept`). The third, `take_ping_pong` (two power-ups taken in turn, each leaving the other where the ant must pass), is **the only guard against a swap livelock** (every Take changes the ant's kind, which renews its progress and resets the count of repeated orders, so neither give-up rule can fire); no test reaches it and it did not fire in the survey. The 16 mutants of the three rules each break one clause (the click, the cost of the ant on its power-up, the Swimmer power-up under a stuck ant, the engine's walk and the bomb it crosses, the defuse from afar and the bomb it crosses, the old rule where the engine's walk knows nothing, the hit points, the landing in water, the second bomb, the stall of 300 ticks, the typed ant) and are all caught, as are the 12 of the later changes (each of the four rules, the dying ant on the planted tile, the switches of the first and the last, the bombs counted as work in the serving test); so is `map_bombs_spared_without_goals` (the breach's `live()` in the counters' sparing, below), which AI29.14 missed on home-next as well until the world had a second Bomber for the counters to act with.

**The counters' sparing outlasts the goals.** `Tactics::gate_goals_live` stays true for the rest of the game once the bot plays the field breach and its task has not given up for good, so after the breach is done the map bombs left near the hill are not countered (on main they are, once the gate goals are exhausted). Left as it is on purpose: it is what keeps the breach's carriers and the next breach's work from being set off.
