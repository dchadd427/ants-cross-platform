🎮 Controls & Field Guide

📱 Touch & Mobile
  • Tap: Select Ant / Move / Attack / Harvest
  • Long Press: Defuse Bomb / Use Ability
  • Minimap Tap: Jump Camera Across Map
  • Fullscreen: Tap ⛶ Full in Header
  • Rotate: Landscape Mode Recommended

🖱️ Basic Controls
  • Left Click: Select Ant / Move / Attack / Harvest
  • Right Click: Move / Defuse Bomb / Use Ability
  • Drag Box: Multi-Ant Marquee Selection
  • Spacebar: Center Camera on Ant or Base

🐜 Switch Teams
  • Ctrl+1 / ⌘1: Team 0 (Green Ants)
  • Ctrl+2 / ⌘2: Team 1 (Red Ants)
  • Ctrl+3 / ⌘3: Team 2 (Blue Ants)
  • Ctrl+4 / ⌘4: Team 3 (Black Ants)
  • Ctrl+Tab / Ctrl+C: Cycle to Next Team
  • Setup Screen: Click team icon to switch

🗺️ Camera & Viewport
  • Arrows / WASD: Pan Viewport
  • Edge Panning: Move mouse to screen edge
  • Minimap: Click to immediately jump camera

⚡ Special Abilities
  • Bomber: Plant bomb (defuses on single click)
  • Fire: Lay impenetrable fire barrier
  • Swimmer: Build & demolish water bridges
  • Thief: Infiltrate enemy anthills

⌨️ Shortcuts
  • Ctrl+T: Toggle 32×32 Tile Grid
  • Ctrl+L: Toggle Unit Health Bars
  • Enter: In-Game Chat Broadcast
  • Esc / F1: In-Game Options & Quick Help
