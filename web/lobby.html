    How it works

    The room

    Open the page and your room is ready, with the first colour yours. The four colours lie as the four hills lie on the maps: Black top left, Green top right, Red bottom left, Blue bottom right. Send the link: players who open it take the next free colour and show up here at once. The host drags a player onto another colour to move them; a colour that is taken swaps places. On a phone, tap a player's dots and then the colour.

    The other colours

    A colour that nobody holds can be left open for a player, given to a computer player (Easy, Medium or Hard) or set to Nobody, which keeps it out of the match.

    Removing a player

    The host can take a player out of the room with the Remove button on their colour. It asks first, and the colour opens up again. The player gets a room of their own, with a note that says why, and can come back by using the link again.

    Teams

    With three or four players (computer players count) you can play in teams: press Team 1 or Team 2 under a player. Two on the same team play together; with four players the other two are the other team. Press a lit button again to take it off. When you move a player to another colour, their team goes along.

    START!

    The host starts the match with whoever is in. An open colour stays empty. When the match is over, Leave Game takes you back to the front page, where a new room is ready.

    When the host leaves

    A room lasts while anybody is in it. If the host leaves, the player who has been in the room longest becomes the host, and everybody is told. When the last player leaves, the room closes about a minute later, and a link to a room that is gone starts a new room for whoever opens it.
