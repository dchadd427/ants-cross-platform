The chat and logging in with Discord

Anyone can read the chat on the front page. To write in it you log in with Discord, which gives the site four things only: your Discord id, username and display name and, if you have set one, your profile picture. They are kept so that the site knows who you are, shows the name that you pick and shows your picture, and for nothing else. The site asks for no email address and no list of friends or servers, and it keeps no Discord access token.

When you log in, the site fetches one small copy of your Discord profile picture from Discord, once, and keeps it on its own server. It is shown to everyone who opens the front page, logged in or not, in the round image next to your name, unless you switch off Show my Discord picture in the menu of your name or the site’s operator hides it. The copy is served by this site under a random address that is new every time the site fetches your picture and holds nothing of your Discord id; other people’s browsers do not contact Discord for it. The site keeps the copy only while you have a login, and for at most 30 days after the login that fetched it. Switching it off, logging out of your last login, or Forget me deletes the copy from the server at once, and so does a kick or a ban; when your last login runs out the copy stops being served at once and is deleted within the hour. Its address can still answer for up to five minutes, and a browser that has already loaded it may keep its own copy for up to a day. Your choice to switch the picture off is remembered, by a code made from your Discord id with a secret key and not by the id, for 400 days after your last login; Forget me deletes that too. If you change your picture at Discord, the site shows the new one after your next login.

Chat lines are kept for up to 7 days and only while they are among the newest 200 lines, with the id and the name that wrote them, so that moderators can deal with abuse, and are then deleted for good. Moderators also see your username next to a display name. A ban or a mute is kept by Discord id.

Moderators and administrators are people who hold a moderator or administrator role in the project’s Discord server. While you are logged in and the chat is open, the site asks Discord, with the project’s bot, whether you hold one of those roles, again every few minutes and when you use a power. The site also asks Discord about any account that a moderator acts on, in the chat or not. The answers are kept in memory only. When a moderator or an administrator mutes, kicks or bans someone, lifts a mute or a ban, or deletes a line, the site writes down who did it, to which account, what it was and when. The accounts in that log are shown by a code that is made from the Discord id with a secret key, so the log itself does not hold the id. It is kept for 30 days, at most 10,000 entries, and is read only by the operator on the server; it is not shown on the site. A kick or a ban is also shown to everyone in the chat as a line with the person’s name, without the moderator’s. Forget me does not remove these entries, nor the line that said you were kicked or banned; that goes with the other lines within 7 days. When the operator hides a picture on the server, that is written down in the same way.

The name of your login is also the name you play under. It is shown in rooms and in the Games and Players lists, and it is written into the match history, which keeps results and names for good, and into the replay files: for 30 days on the site, and for ever in a file that someone has downloaded. Forget me does not remove it from those. The Players list shows the people who are logged in, and whether they are looking around, waiting in a room or playing.

The login sets two cookies, both for logging in and nothing else: __Host-ants_oauth while you are on your way to Discord (10 minutes at most, deleted when you come back) and __Host-ants_hub, a random number that says which login is yours (30 days, deleted when you log out).

The voice button in the chat uses your browser’s speech recognition. Your browser may send the audio to its maker’s speech service (Chrome, Edge and Safari do); this site receives no audio and keeps nothing of it.

Log out in the chat ends the login of this browser. Forget me ends every login of your account, deletes your chat lines that are still kept and removes your username, display name and the copy of your picture from the site’s records; a ban or a mute, if there is one, stays, and so does the operator’s hiding of your picture, kept as one entry under the same kind of code, with no id and no name. Reading the chat and playing need no login. What Discord itself does with your visit is under Discord’s own privacy policy.
