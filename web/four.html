Single game
Waiting for the four games to connect and start...
Every window runs the whole simulation; the server only orders the commands and acts as the referee. Each game tells this page its tick and state hash every 100 ticks (5 seconds): when the four agree, the machines are in step. Click a window to play it (the others stop their music and their sound: only the window you are in is heard). The match is the original's clock: when it ends each window shows its results screen.
